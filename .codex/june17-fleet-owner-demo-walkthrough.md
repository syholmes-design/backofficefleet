# June 17 Fleet-Owner Demo Walkthrough

Created: 2026-06-17
Checklist items: J17-005, J17-011, J17-012
Source: `.codex/client-work-requirements-20260617.md`

## Audience

Primary buyer: a for-hire trucking fleet owner or operations lead with roughly 20-50 trucks. This fleet is large enough to feel document, driver, settlement, dispatch, and proof pressure, but may not have a mature control staff or clean operating layer.

## Demo Objective

Show why BOF is worth hiring: it makes one operating decision easier to trust by keeping the load, driver, carrier, documents, safety, settlement, owner, blocker, consequence, and next action together.

## Presenter Path

### 1. Open the public homepage

Route: `/`

Talk track:

"BOF is not trying to replace the fleet's TMS or dispatch judgment. It sits around the operating records your team already touches, then makes the release answer easier to see: what is cleared, what is blocked, who owns the next action, and what happens if it does not clear."

High points:

- 20-50 truck for-hire fleets are the first buyer focus.
- The owner is buying less chasing, cleaner release decisions, and clearer follow-through.
- Start with one active fleet record, not a broad software tour.

### 2. Enter the demo through the release review

Route: `/interactive-demo/start/`

Talk track:

"This entry screen lets different people inspect the same operating record from their job. I am starting as the fleet owner because ownership needs the fastest answer: can the work move, what money or service risk is attached, and who is responsible?"

Click:

- Start Partner TMS review session, or preview BOF-RR-10482 packet.
- If short on time, choose Fleet owner.

### 3. Show the Command Center first

Route: `/interactive-demo/`

Talk track:

"The owner should not open five systems to understand one release decision. BOF keeps the load state, driver and carrier readiness, document packet, settlement watch, and audit trail together."

High points:

- Ready, watch, and hold states are separated.
- BOF-RR-10482 / TMS-LD-10482 stays the anchor.
- The next action is visible instead of buried in a note.

### 4. Open documents and proof

Route: `/interactive-demo/documents/`

Talk track:

"This is where the pain becomes concrete. Missing PODs, unclear receiver proof, a seal photo, GPS/location detail, dock photo, empty-trailer proof, and claim standby all affect whether settlement, claim response, or release confidence is clean."

Pain points to name:

- Missing PODs.
- Seal mismatch or seal dispute.
- Missing pre-departure documents.
- Missing arrival or post-trip documents.
- Paperwork chaos across email, photos, texts, folders, portals, and TMS screens.
- Fuel or route watch items when they affect cost or delivery confidence.

### 5. Open driver and safety readiness

Routes: `/interactive-demo/drivers/`, `/interactive-demo/safety/`, `/drivers/`, `/safety/`

Talk track:

"A fleet owner does not just need a driver name on a load. They need to know whether the driver file supports the release decision, what is missing, and whether safety or compliance has a real hold."

High points:

- Driver files are inspectable.
- Safety blockers explain the decision.
- Missing or expired items have an owner and next action.

### 6. Show settlements

Routes: `/interactive-demo/settlements/`, `/settlements/`

Talk track:

"Driver pay is not one generic row. BOF can show cents-per-mile, percentage, salary or straight-time logic, gross-to-net, deductions, reimbursements, and proof-controlled holds. The important part is why money is ready, pending, or held."

High points:

- Settlement readiness ties back to POD/BOL/proof.
- Holds are operational, not vague finance delays.
- Drivers have a clearer reason to submit complete proof.

### 7. Close with the right next step

Routes: `/book-demo/` or `/founding-fleet/`

Talk track:

"The first client step should be focused. Bring one active fleet record: a release issue, driver file, POD problem, carrier packet, document chase, settlement hold, fuel/route question, or safety blocker. BOF maps the record chain, owner, consequence, and next move."

Close options:

- Use `/book-demo/` for a normal BOF working session.
- Use `/founding-fleet/` for a qualified early fleet that wants trial access, priority onboarding, and Founding Fleet terms.

## What Not To Do In The Demo

- Do not start with every page.
- Do not describe BOF as a live integration product.
- Do not promise live Mapbox, GPS, RFID, fuel tank, weather, traffic, or telematics.
- Do not turn route/fuel context into the main product story.
- Do not present Founding Fleet as the global story for every prospect.
- Do not spend time on future verticals during a trucking buyer walkthrough.

## One-Sentence Close

"BOF helps a growing trucking fleet stop chasing the same records across people and systems by turning one release decision into a clear operating record with proof, owner, consequence, and next action."
