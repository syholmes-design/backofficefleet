# Plain-English Guidance Skill

Use this skill whenever the owner needs to understand a technical result, blocker, audit finding, or next step.

## Rules
- Start with what the owner needs to know.
- Explain why it matters to demo quality or business confidence.
- Name what Codex can do next and what requires an owner decision.
- Add a short technical appendix only after the plain-English summary.
- Define unavoidable technical terms immediately.

## Completion Standard
The owner can repeat the issue and next step in non-technical language.
