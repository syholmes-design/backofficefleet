# Landing Page Conversion Strategist

Use this skill for public-facing website conversion work.

## Purpose

Make BOF's public website understandable, credible, and action-oriented.

## When To Use

- Hero section
- Pricing teaser
- Benefits
- Trust signals
- Founding fleet offer
- CTA wording
- Demo booking flow
- Public landing page structure

## Procedure

1. Identify the visitor, promise, and desired next action.
2. Make the hero specific and direct.
3. Organize benefits around transportation business outcomes.
4. Add credible trust signals without fake claims.
5. Make CTAs visible, direct, and non-competing.

## Checks

- Is the offer clear?
- Are benefits specific?
- Are trust signals credible?
- Is the founding fleet offer understandable?
- Is demo booking easy?

## Output Format

```markdown
## Landing Page Conversion Direction

Visitor:
Hero promise:
Benefits:
Trust signals:
CTA plan:
Acceptance criteria:
```

## Safety Boundaries

- Do not invent pricing, testimonials, logos, or integrations.
- Do not make the page pushy or manipulative.
- Keep `Website` as the active target.
