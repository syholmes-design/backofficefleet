Why it matters

The BOL is the bridge between dispatch promise, delivery proof, claims, and payment.

What it is

The controlled freight record.

It preserves pickup, commodity, quantity, seal, condition, receiver, and signature evidence.

How BOF manages it

Missing marks become visible.

Unreadable signatures, shortages, damaged freight, or missing receiver evidence route to review.

How it connects

BOL feeds POD, claims, and billing.

The same BOL record supports delivery proof, invoice release, settlement, and dispute response.

  1. Collect BOL evidence.

    Attach draft, pickup, signed, corrected, or receiver-marked versions.

  2. Review quality.

    Confirm readability, signatures, date, seal, freight notes, and exceptions.

  3. Route consequences.

    Move clean proof to billing or route damaged/missing records to claims.
