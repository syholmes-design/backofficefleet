Why it matters

Detention is recoverable only when timing, terms, and approval evidence stay together.

What it is

A controlled accessorial record.

Arrival, departure, delay reason, appointment notes, photos, and customer or broker approval are captured.

How BOF manages it

Terms are checked before billing.

BOF compares detention evidence against the rate confirmation and routes missing approval to the owner.

How it connects

Detention affects invoice and driver pay.

The record feeds billing packets, settlements, customer communication, and exception history.

  1. Capture time evidence.

    Record appointment, arrival, loaded/unloaded, release, and delay reason.

  2. Confirm terms.

    Match free time, rate, approval requirements, and customer/broker instructions.

  3. Release to billing or review.

    Move approved detention to packet preparation or route missing proof to an owner.
