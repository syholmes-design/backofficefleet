Why it matters

Assignment is where customer promise, driver readiness, and operating proof meet.

What it is

The release decision before the load moves.

Dispatch reviews load requirements, driver eligibility, equipment fit, HOS, route notes, proof needs, and owner actions.

How BOF manages it

Ready means explainable.

Each assignment shows the readiness reason, outstanding blockers, and the next action needed to release the load.

How it connects

Assignment feeds proof and billing.

The requirements captured here become the BOL, POD, accessorial, settlement, and exception context later.

  1. Review intake.

    Confirm shipper, lane, freight, appointment, access notes, and proof requirements.

  2. Match capacity.

    Verify driver, equipment, safety, HOS, and pretrip state.

  3. Release or route exception.

    Name the owner and clearance path before work is committed.
