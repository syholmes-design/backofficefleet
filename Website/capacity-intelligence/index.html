Operating boundary

BOF does not act as a broker, dispatcher, freight marketplace, or motor carrier in this demo workflow.

Capacity Intelligence is a readiness and visibility layer. It does not arrange freight, place freight, negotiate freight terms, dispatch loads, or take transportation responsibility.

The module extends the existing BOF system for traditional fleets, carrier networks, small aggregators, and multi-carrier operations while preserving the validated customer portal workflow.
