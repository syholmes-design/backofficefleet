Why the office layer matters

The load can move and the office can still be behind.

Most back-office problems begin as small gaps: an expiring driver record, missing POD, billing hold, safety follow-up, or unclear owner. BOF keeps those gaps attached to the operating record before they become customer, compliance, cash-flow, or management problems.
