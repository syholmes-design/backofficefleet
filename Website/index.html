Artificial intelligence in BOF service delivery

AI assistance helps BOF move the mundane review work out of the manager's hands.

BOF can use artificial intelligence to support the first pass through documents, dates, missing fields, repeated blockers, and readiness signals. That helps BOF surface the likely issue faster so managers spend less time chasing paperwork and more time deciding what should happen next.

Quicker triage
