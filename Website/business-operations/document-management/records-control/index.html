Document Management / Records Control

Classify records by the work they support.

Record control makes a file findable, reviewable, and useful. The office should know whether a document is current, expired, superseded, missing, pending review, or blocking work.

Document ManagementPolicies & Procedures

Record status

Every file needs type, owner, status, and use.

Retention and compliance language should be handled carefully and routed to approved policy controls.

Classification

Group records by workflow.

HR, safety, dispatch, billing, settlement, customer, vendor, policy, and leadership records each need clear purpose.

Routing

Send record issues to the right owner.

Missing, expired, unreadable, incomplete, or superseded documents should produce a clear next action.

Related work

Records control supports reporting and policy follow-through.

The document record should feed the operating view.
