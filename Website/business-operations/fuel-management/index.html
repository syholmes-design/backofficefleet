Fuel Management & Cost Control

Fuel is usually the second-largest trucking expense after driver pay. BOF ties fuel purchases, route decisions, IFTA evidence, idle time, and lane profitability to the operating record.

Where Fuel Control Breaks

SignalWhy It MattersBOF Action
Out-of-route fuelCan erase lane margin and create card misuse concerns.Flag by load, driver, unit, and route variance.
IFTA mismatchMiles and gallons must reconcile by jurisdiction.Match ELD/GPS miles to fuel-card transactions.
Idle exposureBurns fuel and affects maintenance and emissions.Show idle trend by unit and coaching owner.
DEF exceptionMissing DEF or emissions events can cause derate.Track DEF purchases and emissions alerts by unit.

The fuel page should eventually support route-linked fuel station recommendations, fuel-price deltas, card-network limits, and projected savings. Until live APIs are connected, the demo can show the decision framework with sample station, cost, and route data.
