Operational integration

Every issue needs a reason, owner, due date, operational consequence, and clearance path.

Administrative data only matters when it changes a fleet decision. BOF keeps the office record tied to what operations can release, hold, pay, bill, or escalate.

Reason

Why is the item not ready?

Missing W-9, payroll setup gap, tax support, stale vendor approval, unmatched receipt, or unresolved benefits issue.

Owner

Who owns the next action?

HR owner, payroll desk, finance lead, AP reviewer, vendor manager, or BOF support path is named before work drifts.

Consequence

What fleet decision is affected?

Driver readiness, vendor use, settlement release, customer billing, cash-flow forecast, or leadership review.

Clearance

What clears the hold?

The record shows the document, approval, policy step, payment check, or service support required to move forward.
