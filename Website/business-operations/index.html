Primary workspace

The office work behind fleet execution belongs in one reviewable operating layer.

Business Operations is not a second settlement page. It covers the administrative questions that affect readiness: worker files, payroll setup, AP review, receivables follow-up, vendor status, office blockers, and owner action.

The page is built for an office manager, controller, HR owner, or fleet leader who needs to know which internal item is slowing work down, who owns it, and whether the consequence is people readiness, vendor use, bookkeeping, customer follow-up, or leadership reporting.

Operating consequence

Back-office control protects the fleet from preventable slowdowns.

When people records, finance approvals, vendor documents, and office exceptions live beside operating work, owners can see what is ready, what is due, what is blocked, and what follow-up must happen next.

The practical value is less rework: fewer mystery holds, fewer unmanaged approvals, and fewer handoffs where the office knows there is a problem but operations cannot see the next responsible owner.
