Administrative Operating Layer

Five Systems. One Unified Operating Layer.

Fleet owners and aggregators do not just move freight. They operate businesses with complex tax obligations, multi-entity accounting, workforce compliance, and payroll structures that no generic software was built to handle. BOF integrates Excise Taxes, Accounting, ERP, HR, and Payroll into a single administrative layer purpose-built for trucking. Every record stays connected to fleet execution.

TX

Excise Taxes & Federal Compliance

Trucking carriers face a web of federal and state excise tax obligations that change with mileage, weight, routes, and fuel consumption. Missing a Form 2290 filing, IFTA quarterly report, or IRP renewal can ground your fleet. BOF tracks every obligation, deadline, and filing status so nothing slips.

  • Form 2290 / HVUT filing and stamped Schedule 1 tracking
  • IFTA quarterly fuel tax reporting across all jurisdictions
  • IRP apportioned registration and cab card management
  • UCR annual registration and state weight-distance tax compliance
