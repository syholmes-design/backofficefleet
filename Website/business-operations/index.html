Excise Taxes & Federal Compliance

Trucking carriers face a web of federal and state excise tax obligations that change with mileage, weight, routes, and fuel consumption. Missing a Form 2290 filing, IFTA quarterly report, or IRP renewal can ground your fleet. BOF tracks every obligation, deadline, and filing status so nothing slips.

  • Form 2290 / HVUT filing and stamped Schedule 1 tracking
  • IFTA quarterly fuel tax reporting across all jurisdictions
  • IRP apportioned registration and cab card management
  • UCR annual registration and state weight-distance tax compliance
Explore Excise Taxes →

Accounting & Financial Operations

Trucking accounting requires per-mile cost tracking, load-level profitability, factoring reconciliation, and driver settlement accounting that general-purpose platforms do not handle cleanly. BOF structures your financials around how freight actually moves. Your books reflect your lanes, not just your bank account.

  • Trucking-specific chart of accounts for revenue, fuel, maintenance, and driver pay
  • Load-level and lane-level P&L visibility
  • Factoring reconciliation and advance tracking
  • AP for fuel cards, vendor invoices, and owner-operator settlements
Explore Accounting →

HR

Human Resources & DOT Compliance

Hiring, onboarding, and managing CDL drivers is a DOT-regulated function where every personnel file must contain specific documentation. BOF manages the compliance layer your HR platform was never built for, including DQF, drug and alcohol program records, medical cards, MVRs, and Clearinghouse queries.

  • Driver Qualification File assembly and ongoing monitoring
  • DOT drug and alcohol program tracking
  • CDL, medical certificate, and license expiration alerts
  • New hire onboarding workflows tied to dispatch readiness
Explore HR →
