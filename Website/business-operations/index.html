Primary workspace

The office work behind fleet execution belongs in one reviewable operating layer and service path.

Business Operations is not a second settlement page. It covers the administrative questions that affect readiness: worker files, payroll setup, AP review, receivables follow-up, vendor status, office blockers, and owner action.

The page is built for an office manager, controller, HR owner, or fleet leader who needs to know which internal item is slowing work down, who owns it, what BOF-supported service work can help move it, and whether the consequence is people readiness, vendor use, bookkeeping, customer follow-up, or leadership reporting.

Office exception queue

Every issue needs a reason, owner, due date, operational consequence, and clearance path.

The queue is intentionally compact. Filter the office work, then open the reason and the route that clears review, at-risk, due, or blocked status.

Review
Missing vendor W-9

AP owner · due Jul 18 · vendor payment cannot be approved

Open vendor record

Why this is under review: The vendor packet is usable for operations, but payment release requires a current W-9 and payee confirmation.

  1. 1Request W-9 from vendor.

    BOF support or AP owner sends the packet request and attaches the response.

  2. 2Confirm payee and tax record.

    AP owner verifies name, EIN, remit address, and approved vendor status.

  3. 3Clear payment hold.

    Finance lead marks vendor approved and releases the bill for normal AP review.

At Risk
Payroll setup unresolved

People ops · due Jul 19 · worker cannot be released to payroll run

Open worker file

Why this is at risk: Worker onboarding is substantially complete, but payroll setup lacks the final pay basis and direct-deposit confirmation.

  1. 1Confirm pay basis and start date.

    HR owner validates employee or contractor setup against assignment readiness.

  2. 2Attach payroll support record.

    BOF support indexes the setup note, tax form status, and direct-deposit confirmation.

  3. 3Release to payroll queue.

    Payroll owner confirms the worker can enter the next pay run.

In Progress
Unapproved expense class

Finance lead · due Jul 21 · bookkeeping review remains incomplete

Open finance review

Why this is in progress: The receipt is attached, but the expense class and operating purpose need owner approval before bookkeeping closes the item.

  1. 1Match receipt to operating record.

    Connect the charge to vendor, unit, load, department, or office owner.

  2. 2Approve classification.

    Finance lead selects repair, fuel, toll, office, reimbursement, or exception category.

  3. 3Close bookkeeping review.

    Record moves from owner review to ready for posting.

Due
Contract renewal due

Office manager · due Jul 24 · vendor status moves to review

Open renewal

Why this is due: The vendor is still usable, but the renewal window is open and the office needs approval before the record ages into review.

  1. 1Confirm current contract terms.

    Office manager reviews expiration date, scope, rates, and renewal owner.

  2. 2Attach renewal evidence.

    BOF support attaches contract note, approval email, or updated vendor document.

  3. 3Update vendor status.

    Record returns to current or moves to review with a named blocker.

Blocked
Employee onboarding incomplete

HR owner · due Jul 25 · assignment readiness remains blocked

Open onboarding

Why this is blocked: The person cannot be released into active work because required identity, tax, policy, and training acknowledgments are incomplete.

  1. 1Collect missing onboarding items.

    HR owner or BOF support requests identity, tax, policy, and training acknowledgments.

  2. 2Confirm policy and training status.

    People ops validates required acknowledgments and any manager review notes.

  3. 3Release assignment readiness.

    Status moves from blocked to review or ready after HR owner signoff.

Operating consequence

Back-office control protects the fleet from preventable slowdowns.

When people records, finance approvals, vendor documents, and office exceptions live beside operating work, owners can see what is ready, what is due, what is blocked, what BOF can support, and what follow-up must happen next.

The practical value is less rework: fewer mystery holds, fewer unmanaged approvals, and fewer handoffs where the office knows there is a problem but operations cannot see the next responsible owner.
