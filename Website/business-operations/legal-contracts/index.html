Legal & Contract Management

Motor carriers operate inside broker, shipper, equipment, owner-operator, vendor, and insurance contracts. BOF does not give legal advice; it makes contract records, renewal dates, risk terms, owners, and dispute evidence visible.

Contract Clearance Path

  1. Capture agreement. Current signed copy, amendment, renewal date, and owner are stored.
  2. Extract terms. Insurance, payment, service, claim, notice, and renewal provisions become review fields.
  3. Assign review. Business owner, legal reviewer, insurance agent, or finance owner gets the next action.
  4. Attach evidence. Approval note, revised term, notice sent, or dispute outcome stays with the record.
