Authority control

Operating Authority & FMCSA Portal Management

Authority status is the license to operate. BOF tracks USDOT updates, MC authority, BOC-3 process agent records, UCR filings, portal access, insurance filings, and renewal ownership.

Authority Health Checklist

ControlRecordRisk if Missed
USDOT numberMCS-150 update cycle, census data, contact ownerOutdated filing and compliance exposure.
MC authorityActive status, commodity type, reinstatement recordLoads cannot lawfully move under inactive authority.
BOC-3Process agent filing and provider detailAuthority application or reinstatement delay.
UCRAnnual registration and payment evidenceState enforcement and registration hold.
Portal accessAuthorized users, MFA, role ownerFiling and audit response blocked by access gap.

Authority Clearance Path

  1. Identify filing. Authority, insurance, UCR, MCS-150, or portal access issue is opened.
  2. Name owner. Compliance owner, insurance agent, portal admin, or leadership owner is assigned.
  3. Attach evidence. Filing confirmation, receipt, screenshot, or agency communication is stored.
  4. Release consequence. BOF shows whether dispatch, carrier onboarding, or broker approval is affected.
