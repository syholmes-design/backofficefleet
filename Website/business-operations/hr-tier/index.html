Business Operations / HR Tier

Keep people records connected to the fleet workflow.

The HR Tier is where BOF tracks the administrative work behind recruiting, onboarding, training, benefits coordination, worker files, and owner follow-up before those issues become dispatch or payroll blockers.

What this page owns

HR work has to show status, owner, and operating impact.

BOF does not treat HR as a loose office checklist. The HR Tier explains which person, document, acknowledgement, or follow-up item affects readiness.

Recruiting intake

Track candidate source, application status, screening step, missing information, and the person responsible for the next contact.

Worker file readiness

Keep tax, payroll, emergency contact, training, acknowledgement, and policy records visible beside driver or staff readiness.

Training and acknowledgement

Show whether required procedures, safety acknowledgements, and operating expectations have been reviewed and accepted.

Owner follow-up

Assign each blocker to a role so the office knows whether HR, safety, dispatch, finance, or leadership needs to act.
