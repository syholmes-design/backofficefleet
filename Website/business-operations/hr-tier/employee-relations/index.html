HR Tier / Employee Relations

Keep people issues visible before they become operating drag.

Employee relations in trucking touches attendance, communication, conduct, manager follow-up, return-to-work context, policy acknowledgement, and retention. BOF organizes those signals without turning the website into legal advice or replacing HR judgment.

Operating concern

Every issue needs a fact pattern, owner, and next review point.

For fleet operators, the office needs to know whether the matter affects safety, assignment readiness, payroll support, customer service, or retention risk.

Case intake

Capture the concern clearly.

Record who raised the issue, what happened, related route or terminal context, relevant documents, immediate risk, and the owner responsible for review.

  • Attendance and call-off pattern
  • Customer or manager complaint
  • Policy acknowledgement gap
  • Return-to-work follow-up
Operating impact

Separate HR review from fleet consequence.

BOF helps show whether the matter blocks dispatch, triggers safety review, affects payroll, needs manager coaching, or can stay in routine follow-up.

  • Ready, review, blocked status
  • Named owner and due date
  • Related document or policy
  • Escalation path
