Onboarding path

Every onboarding step should point to the next operating action.

A driver is not ready just because a form exists. BOF shows what is complete, what is missing, who owns the next step, and whether dispatch can assign the driver.

Application intake

Collect the candidate profile, contact record, experience notes, prior employer context, and missing intake items.

DQF handoff

Connect driver qualification requirements to the Driver Command Center so missing credentials appear as readiness blockers.

Payroll setup

Track pay basis, direct deposit or contractor packet status, tax form status, and internal review owner.

Assignment release

Show whether safety acknowledgement, training, equipment status, and driver record status allow assignment.
