Human Resources & DOT Compliance for Motor Carriers

Managing people in a trucking company is federally regulated work. BOF connects recruiting, onboarding, Driver Qualification Files, drug and alcohol records, training, policy acknowledgments, and readiness status to whether a driver can safely be assigned.

What Makes Trucking HR Different

Standard HR platforms manage hiring, benefits, PTO, reviews, and separation. Trucking HR must also maintain Driver Qualification Files under 49 CFR Part 391, drug and alcohol testing records under 49 CFR Part 382, and recurring monitoring for medical certificates, MVRs, and Clearinghouse queries.

The ongoing monitoring burden is where many carriers fall short. Annual MVR pulls, annual Clearinghouse limited queries, staggered medical card renewals, random testing pool management, and training acknowledgments create a calendar that never stops.

Onboarding-to-Dispatch Workflow

  1. Application & Initial Screening. Employment history, license status, initial MVR, lane fit, and disqualifying offense screen.
  2. Conditional Offer. Background authorization, PSP consent, Clearinghouse consent, and query initiation.
  3. Pre-Employment Drug Test. Test ordered through the appropriate program; negative result recorded before dispatch release.
  4. Document Collection. CDL, medical card, previous employer safety history, PSP, and policy acknowledgments attached.
  5. DQF Assembly. Safety owner reviews the file and records sign-off or missing evidence.
  6. System Setup. ELD, TMS profile, emergency contact, payroll/settlement profile, and training assignment created.
  7. First Dispatch Cleared. Driver is active only after DQF, testing, medical certificate, and readiness status support assignment.

Ongoing Monitoring Calendar

BOF keeps the recurring calendar visible: annual MVR, annual Clearinghouse limited query, medical certificate renewal, license status, random testing pool, training, and policy acknowledgment. Each date has an owner, consequence, and clearance path.
