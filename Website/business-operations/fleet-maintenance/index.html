Fleet Maintenance & Asset Management

Part 396 requires a systematic inspection, repair, and maintenance program. BOF turns PM intervals, annual inspections, DVIR defects, tire records, and roadside repairs into dispatch-visible clearance gates.

Maintenance Records That Affect Release

ControlOperating RecordClearance Rule
Preventive maintenanceMileage, date, engine-hours interval, work order, vendor invoiceAlert before due; hold unit when overdue or safety-critical.
DOT annual inspectionInspection certificate, sticker date, inspector credentialExpired annual inspection blocks dispatch until certificate is current.
DVIR defectDriver report, defect category, repair certificationSafety-affecting defect must be repaired and certified before release.
Tire programPosition, tread depth, pressure, replacement, retread eligibilityUnsafe tread or pressure exception routes to maintenance owner.

Breakdown Response Workflow

  1. Driver notice. Driver reports location, unit, symptoms, and load impact.
  2. Vendor dispatch. Approved repair vendor, ETA, estimate, and spending authority are recorded.
  3. Load decision. Dispatch decides whether to wait, relay, rescue, or notify the customer.
  4. Return to service. Repair invoice, post-repair inspection, and new DVIR clear the unit.
