Safety Management & SMS Compliance

SMS scores are business signals. BOF connects roadside inspections, violations, crashes, DataQ challenges, driver qualification, corrective action, and dispatch consequence in one reviewable record.

What the Safety Page Must Answer

QuestionBOF EvidenceOperating Consequence

SMS BASIC Review

Unsafe Driving, Crash Indicator, HOS Compliance, Vehicle Maintenance, Controlled Substances/Alcohol, Hazardous Materials, and Driver Fitness must be tracked by cause and consequence. BOF does not just show a score; it shows the reason the score moved and the correction path.

  1. Capture the event. Inspection or telematics signal attaches to driver, unit, and load.
  2. Classify the exposure. BASIC, severity, preventability, and operational risk are recorded.
  3. Assign corrective action. Coaching, document correction, maintenance repair, or DataQ challenge is opened.
  4. Close with proof. Completion evidence stays with the record before release language changes.
