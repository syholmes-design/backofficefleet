Government / Single Record Accountability

One operating record should answer who cleared the move and why.

Government and contract fleet work breaks down when dispatch, safety, documents, and billing each hold a different answer. BOF keeps the release decision, evidence, owner, and follow-up in the same record.

Review lens

Can this route, driver, equipment, and packet be released?

The answer needs more than a yes or no. It needs a record that shows document status, exception owner, timestamp, and the clearance path.

Dispatch

Release status

Route, service window, driver assignment, unit assignment, delivery constraints, and current hold state.

Safety

Clearance gate

Credential validity, inspection evidence, HOS availability, incident flags, and policy acknowledgement.

Documents

Packet proof

Rate terms, BOL, insurance evidence, access instructions, receiver notes, and required signatures.

Owner

Next action

Named owner, due date, resolution note, and whether the hold affects dispatch, proof, settlement, or reporting.

How the record moves

  1. Capture: pull driver, load, equipment, policy, and proof requirements into one working record.
  2. Classify: mark ready, review, held, or blocked with a reason that follows the record.
  3. Clear: assign the missing item to an owner and record the evidence that resolved it.
  4. Release: preserve the release note, timestamp, reviewer, and downstream settlement impact.
