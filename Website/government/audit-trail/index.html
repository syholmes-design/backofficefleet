Audit lens

Can a manager reconstruct the decision without asking five people what happened?

Audit readiness is not just storage. It is a readable sequence of evidence, ownership, review, and clearance.

Status

Clear designation

Ready, review, held, blocked, or released with a reason that follows the operating record.

Evidence

Attached proof

Documents, photos, notes, acknowledgements, approvals, and packet changes are tied to the issue they cleared.

Ownership

Named owner

The person or role responsible for the next action is visible with due date, review note, and escalation path.

Impact

Downstream effect

Dispatch, safety, proof, settlement, and reporting impact are visible before the issue becomes a surprise.

Audit trail sequence

  1. Log: capture the issue, source record, current status, and why the item is not ready.
  2. Own: assign the next action to a person, role, or department with due date and effect.
  3. Resolve: attach the document, review note, approval, or corrected evidence.
  4. Preserve: keep the final release state readable for later manager, agency, or contract review.
