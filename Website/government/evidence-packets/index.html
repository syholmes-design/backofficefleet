The packet should show the proof before the question arrives.

Packet lens

What proof is present, missing, expired, disputed, or waiting on an owner?

Evidence is useful only when it is tied to the route, driver, release decision, and settlement effect.

Credentials

Driver and carrier proof

License, medical card, insurance evidence, carrier authority, site access, and role-specific acknowledgements.

Trip evidence

BOL and delivery proof

BOL draft, signed BOL, POD, receiver notes, seal record, dock photo, cargo photo, and time stamps.

Equipment

Inspection and readiness

Pre-trip inspection, trailer condition, tire/equipment photos, required gear, and release-impacting defects.

Exception

Clearance route

Missing proof is marked with a reason, owner, due date, and the action needed before release or invoice.

Evidence packet checklist

  1. Required: name every document needed for this agency, contractor, route, and service type.
  2. Received: show whether each item is attached, readable, current, and linked to the right record.
  3. Reviewed: capture reviewer, note, exception status, and policy rule used to clear or hold the packet.
  4. Released: preserve the packet state that supported dispatch, delivery proof, billing, and audit review.
