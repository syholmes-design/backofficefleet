Operating model

Record. Reason. Owner. Next action.

BOF organizes the evidence and responsibility behind fleet decisions so the office can see what is ready, what is blocked, and who can move it next.

Mission

Make fleet operations easier to verify, assign, and move forward.

BOF is built for teams that cannot afford scattered proof, unclear ownership, or a status that hides the reason behind it. The mission is practical: help fleet work become more controlled, more visible, and easier to act on.

Operating problem

The office layer often becomes the bottleneck.

Dispatch can be ready while proof is missing. A driver can be assigned while a file needs review. Billing can wait on a POD, claim note, or accessorial receipt. BOF keeps those dependencies visible.

BOF answer

Connect the record to the action.

Each workflow is framed around the record, the evidence, the owner, the risk, and the next step. That keeps service work and software visibility tied to the same operating facts.
