Aggregator readiness

Why Aggregators Need BOF

BOF provides operational infrastructure for carrier networks and aggregators. It helps turn scattered carrier information into a readiness view that supports growth without creating a separate aggregator product or exposing private BOF customer relationships.

Network growth creates back-office pressure.

Aggregators grow by adding carrier relationships. Each new relationship adds more drivers, assets, insurance records, settlements, claims, onboarding tasks, and document follow-up. BOF helps convert that scattered information into operational readiness.

Visibility can protect the network.

BOF helps aggregators see capacity readiness without forcing every carrier into the same operating structure. The view supports readiness, documentation, compliance, and administrative workflows while protecting private carrier and customer relationship details.

Infrastructure, not competition.

Aggregators can use BOF as infrastructure for readiness management, carrier follow-up, claims coordination, and settlement support. BOF does not act as a broker, dispatcher, freight marketplace, or motor carrier in this demo workflow.
