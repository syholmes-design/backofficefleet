Driver Workflow

Driver onboarding that ends in assignment readiness.

BOF treats onboarding as an operating gate, not a paperwork folder. The driver profile, qualification file, policy acknowledgements, payroll setup, training status, and dispatch release notes stay connected until the driver can actually take work.

Why it matters

A driver is not ready until safety, payroll, documents, and dispatch can all rely on the same record.

What it is

One intake path for the driver record.

Application details, contact data, license class, tax setup, equipment needs, and first-assignment notes enter a controlled workflow.

How BOF manages it

Every missing item carries an owner.

Credential review, policy acknowledgement, payroll setup, and training exceptions show status, reason, owner, and clearance path.

How it connects

Onboarding feeds dispatch and settlement.

The same record determines assignment eligibility, document readiness, communication context, and payment setup.

  1. Collect the profile.

    Capture identity, contact, experience, equipment, pay setup, and onboarding owner.

  2. Clear required evidence.

    Review license, medical card, MVR, training, policy acknowledgements, and payroll requirements.

  3. Release to operations.

    Show dispatch whether the driver is ready, under review, or blocked before assignment.
