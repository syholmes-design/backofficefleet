Why it matters

A message is useful only when the office can see the record, reason, and next action.

What it is

Driver support with operating context.

Messages are grouped around onboarding, active loads, proof needs, compliance blockers, settlement holds, and policy acknowledgements.

How BOF manages it

Every thread has a next action.

Support items identify owner, linked record, requested evidence, and whether the issue affects release, billing, or pay.

How it connects

Communication closes workflow gaps.

Driver support routes back to dispatch, safety, documents, settlement, and exception management.

  1. Attach the message.

    Link the question to a driver, load, proof packet, document, or settlement item.

  2. Name the operating effect.

    Show whether the issue is informational, review required, blocked, or ready to close.

  3. Close the loop.

    Capture owner response, evidence request, status change, and follow-up history.
