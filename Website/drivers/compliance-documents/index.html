Why it matters

Compliance failures become operating delays when the release reason is hidden.

What it is

A living driver evidence file.

The driver record shows what is current, missing, expiring, disputed, or waiting for review.

How BOF manages it

Status with reason and owner.

Each review item identifies the blocker, responsible owner, due date, and the action needed to clear it.

How it connects

Safety controls dispatch eligibility.

Dispatch sees the release effect of compliance issues without searching a separate safety folder.

  1. Receive evidence.

    Documents are collected from driver, safety, office, or intake channels.

  2. Classify and review.

    BOF records document type, effective dates, driver match, and review status.

  3. Expose release effect.

    Ready, review, or blocked states become visible to dispatch and management.
