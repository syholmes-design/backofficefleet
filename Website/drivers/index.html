Fleet-owned driver files

Each driver record has the paperwork a fleet owner expects to inspect.

BOF separates the fleet's own driver qualification files from outside carrier packet records. A carrier can be attached to the load, but BOF does not pretend the fleet owner controls another company's employee file.
