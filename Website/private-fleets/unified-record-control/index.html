Unified record route

  1. Collect: connect driver, branch, route, unit, packet, and proof requirements.
  2. Score: mark ready, review, held, or blocked with a reason that follows the record.
  3. Assign: route the missing item to the correct owner before dispatch guesses.
  4. Release: keep the release decision readable for operations, billing, and management review.
