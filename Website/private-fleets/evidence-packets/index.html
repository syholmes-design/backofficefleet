Packet lens

Is the proof complete enough to release, bill, defend, or escalate?

A packet is not just storage. It is the evidence trail that explains why the move was trusted.

Before release

Readiness evidence

Driver file, unit readiness, route instructions, branch notes, and access requirements.

During move

Trip proof

BOL, seal, cargo condition, dock note, equipment/tire photo, and exception capture.

After delivery

Delivery closeout

POD, receiver confirmation, timestamp, detention note, damage note, and dispute flag.

Finance

Billing support

Proof packet, internal chargeback context, customer reference, and settlement impact.

Evidence packet flow

  1. Define: name the proof required for this route, branch, service type, and customer.
  2. Attach: link photos, documents, notes, and acknowledgements to the move.
  3. Review: show what is missing, unreadable, expired, or waiting on a role.
  4. Close: preserve the proof state used for release, billing, dispute, or audit review.
