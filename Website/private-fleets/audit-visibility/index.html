Pattern

Repeat blockers

Recurring proof gaps, branch delays, document misses, and owner queues become visible.

Audit visibility route

  1. Record: capture the issue, current state, evidence, and affected work.
  2. Explain: show the reason and the route to clear it in plain operational language.
  3. Resolve: attach the fix, timestamp, reviewer, and release effect.
  4. Improve: review repeat blockers by branch, driver, document type, route, or owner.
