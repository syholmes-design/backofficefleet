Sample SOP library

Procedure summaries show how the policy library connects to daily operating records.

These excerpts are illustrative examples for public-site review. BOF can help organize policy references, owners, dates, records, and workflow connections, while fleets keep their own legal, safety, HR, accounting, and security review where required.

DriversReview required

Driver Qualification SOP

Owner
Driver file owner
Cadence
Hire, annual review, credential change
Records
CDL, medical card, MVR, application, road test
Trigger
New driver, renewal, file gap, safety review
  1. Confirm required DQF records are present or flagged before dispatch assignment.
  2. Route missing credentials to the driver record owner for follow-up.
  3. Record reviewer notes when a file is not ready for release.

Sample excerpt: A driver file is marked ready only after the assigned reviewer can see required credential records or a documented exception requiring fleet review.

Missing-document consequence: dispatch readiness stays in review until the owner resolves or documents the gap.

SafetyProcedure sample

Cargo Securement Procedure

Owner
Safety or operations lead
Cadence
Before release, after exception
Records
Load type, securement notes, seal/photo proof
Trigger
High-risk cargo, customer rule, claim concern
  1. Capture cargo type, securement expectation, and required photo or seal evidence.
  2. Assign an owner when proof is missing or unclear.
  3. Hold the exception note with the load packet for later claims review.

Sample excerpt: Pre-trip release should show the securement method, required proof, exception note, and owner responsible for resolving missing evidence.

Missing-document consequence: release can be marked review needed until the proof item or exception owner is visible.

HOS reviewConcept support

HOS Review Procedure

Owner
Dispatch or safety reviewer
Cadence
Before assignment, route delay, exception
Records
Route timing, available-hours note, delay reason
Trigger
Long haul, delay, relay, reviewer concern
  1. Review the fleet-supplied HOS or ELD-style information available to the team.
  2. Document whether route timing or delay information needs human review.
  3. Keep the reviewer action with the dispatch record.

Sample excerpt: BOF can support review of route timing and available-hours concerns when the fleet supplies the underlying information.

Missing-document consequence: the load record shows an unresolved review item rather than implying the timing issue was cleared.

IncidentEscalation path

Accident Reporting Procedure

Owner
Safety lead
Cadence
Incident event, claim review, closeout
Records
Driver report, location, photos, contacts
Trigger
Accident, roadside event, customer notice
  1. Open an incident record with time, location, driver report, and available photos.
  2. Capture police, customer, insurer, and internal owner contact notes.
  3. Flag related settlement, billing, or claims holds before closeout.

Sample excerpt: Accident reporting stays open until the safety owner confirms evidence, contacts, and downstream holds are visible.

Missing-document consequence: the record remains in exception status for safety-owner review.

EvidencePacket check

Claims Evidence Procedure

Owner
Claims or billing owner
Cadence
Damage notice, shortage, claim hold
Records
POD, BOL, seal, dock, empty trailer, messages
Trigger
Customer dispute, carrier claim, billing hold
  1. Group proof items before billing or payout release.
  2. Identify the missing evidence item and owner.
  3. Keep customer communication and claim-owner notes together.

Sample excerpt: Claims evidence should show what happened, what proof exists, what is missing, and which owner is responsible for next review.

Missing-document consequence: billing, factoring, or settlement release can show a hold note tied to the claim.

BillingBilling ready

Customer Billing Procedure

Owner
Billing owner
Cadence
Delivered load, accessorial request, invoice review
Records
Rate confirmation, POD, lumper, detention, invoice
Trigger
Delivery complete, customer exception, AR review
  1. Confirm the packet includes rate, proof, accessorial, and invoice support.
  2. Route missing POD, lumper, or detention proof to the assigned owner.
  3. Record whether the packet is billing ready or exception pending.

Sample excerpt: A billing packet is marked ready when the billing owner can see the records needed to explain the invoice.

Missing-document consequence: invoice readiness shows an exception until the missing proof is attached or documented.

SettlementsApproval review

Settlement Approval Procedure

Owner
Settlement reviewer
Cadence
Pay cycle, adjustment, claim hold
Records
Pay basis, reimbursements, deductions, holds
Trigger
Settlement run, driver dispute, billing exception
  1. Check pay basis, reimbursements, deductions, and contractor support.
  2. Confirm whether safety holds or claims holds affect release.
  3. Record approval, review needed, or blocked status before payout recommendation.

Sample excerpt: Settlement approval should explain both the payment inputs and the unresolved exceptions that affect release.

Missing-document consequence: the packet stays pending until the reviewer can see the support or the exception note.

RecordsLibrary policy

Records Retention Policy

Owner
Business operations owner
Cadence
Quarterly, annual, customer or legal review
Records
Driver, dispatch, safety, billing, settlement, HR
Trigger
New record type, audit prep, retention question
  1. Assign each record group a location, owner, retention cue, and access boundary.
  2. Flag stale or unclear retention notes for professional review.
  3. Keep completion status visible to the business owner.

Sample excerpt: Retention references should help the fleet find records and identify owner review needs, not replace legal retention guidance.

Missing-document consequence: records without an owner or location remain incomplete in the library.

SecurityAccess review

Information Security Policy

Owner
Business or security owner
Cadence
Quarterly, access change, incident review
Records
Access roles, sensitive files, financial records
Trigger
New user, role change, vendor change, concern
  1. Classify sensitive operating records and limit access to appropriate roles.
  2. Record access-owner decisions and review dates.
  3. Avoid exposing protected driver, financial, or private operating data in public examples.

Sample excerpt: Sensitive records should be handled through controlled roles, review notes, and owner-approved access paths.

Missing-document consequence: access questions stay flagged until the assigned owner reviews the record group.

CybersecurityControl reference

Cybersecurity Procedure

Owner
Security or technology owner
Cadence
Quarterly, incident, account change
Records
Password, MFA, remote access, device, vendor account
Trigger
New system, user offboarding, suspected event
  1. Track account, authentication, device, and remote-access procedure references.
  2. Route suspected events to the assigned response owner.
  3. Record vendor-account follow-up without implying prevention is guaranteed.

Sample excerpt: BOF can help organize cybersecurity procedure references and owner follow-up, while security professionals remain responsible for technical controls.

Missing-document consequence: unresolved access or incident notes remain open for owner review.

ContinuityRecovery reference

Business Continuity Procedure

Owner
Operations leader
Cadence
Annual, vendor change, disruption review
Records
Critical contacts, backup locations, manual steps
Trigger
System outage, weather event, staffing gap
  1. List critical operating owners, backup contacts, and recovery priorities.
  2. Document manual-workaround notes for dispatch, billing, settlement, and records work.
  3. Review continuity references after disruptions or major vendor changes.

Sample excerpt: Continuity procedures keep recovery ownership visible when normal back-office tools or staff coverage are interrupted.

Missing-document consequence: backup responsibility remains unclear until the operating owner updates the procedure.

Boundary language

BOF helps organize, surface, and maintain visibility around policies and procedures. BOF does not provide legal advice, replace legal counsel, certify compliance, automatically satisfy FMCSA or DOT requirements, guarantee regulatory approval, eliminate liability, or guarantee protection from cybersecurity events.

Flag gaps

Missing owner, stale date, unclear approval path, or unsupported record can become a review item.

Support accountability

Policies become part of the same operating record discipline as drivers, dispatch, safety, settlements, and business operations.
