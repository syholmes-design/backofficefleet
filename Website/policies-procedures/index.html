Controlled Policy Library

The current approved version should be obvious, but history should remain inspectable.

A controlled library separates draft, approved, pending acknowledgment, training required, corrective action, superseded, and archived states with plain-language consequences.

Current approved versionThe version governing the assigned audience now.
Superseded versionHistorical evidence preserved after a newer approval.
DraftNot published; review and approval still pending.
Archived policyNo longer current, but retained for audit history.

Policy Workflow

Draft -> Review -> Approve -> Publish -> Assign -> Acknowledge -> Train -> Monitor -> Revise -> Archive

Each step preserves the owner, state, evidence, and next action so a written policy becomes an auditable operating control.

Acknowledgment and Training Workflow

Acknowledgment evidence matters most when someone asks who received the rule.

Pending acknowledgment

The assigned person or group still needs to acknowledge the current approved version.

Training required

The policy has a linked training requirement, owner, due date, and completion evidence.

Corrective action

An exception routes to a named owner and remains visible until the resolution note is preserved.

Approval status

Draft and revision records remain separate from the current approved operating rule.

Exceptions and Corrective Action

Exceptions should name the policy, person, owner, deadline, consequence, and resolution path.

BOF supports compliance oversight by keeping the policy record, acknowledgment gap, training requirement, corrective action, and retained evidence together for review.

Exception opened: billing packet acknowledgment overdue Owner assigned: billing supervisor Corrective action: complete packet training Consequence: packet release requires owner review Resolution evidence: acknowledgment and training note
