Drivers / v3.2 / Effective Jul 1, 2026

Driver Qualification Policy

Purpose: prevent dispatch from treating a driver as available until the driver file, qualification evidence, and owner review support active assignment.

Owner
Driver file owner
Approver
Safety lead
Review cadence
Quarterly plus annual MVR cycle
Applies to
Company, contractor, and owner-operator drivers

Operating Standard

A driver must not be marked ready unless license status, medical qualification, MVR review, application, DQF checklist, required acknowledgments, and assignment eligibility are current or formally cleared by the safety owner.

Required Evidence

  • CDL or equivalent license record and current medical qualification status.
  • MVR pull/review note, annual review status, and reviewer name.
  • Driver application, DQF checklist, road test/CDL equivalency where applicable.
  • Safety, training, policy, and operating acknowledgments.

Exception Path

Expired, missing, unreadable, disputed, or pending documents keep the driver under review or blocked. The clearance route must show missing item, owner, next action, due date, and ready-again condition.

Operating consequenceDriver roster status, dispatch release, HOS readiness, and assignment eligibility must reflect the driver-file state.Revision note: v3.2 adds ready-again condition and owner signoff requirement.
Safety / v2.4 / Effective Jun 15, 2026

Pre-Trip Evidence Standard

Purpose: make dispatch release depend on visible evidence when equipment, cargo condition, tire condition, seal control, or securement proof affects the load.

Owner
Safety lead
Approver
Operations lead
Review cadence
Monthly exception review
Applies to
Loads requiring pre-trip or release proof

Operating Standard

Dispatch may not treat the load as fully releasable until the pre-trip packet shows inspection status, unit readiness, required cargo/seal/securement proof, and any safety review state.

Required Evidence

  • Pre-trip inspection state and defect/repair status if reported.
  • Tire, equipment, cargo, seal, and securement photos when required by lane, customer, claim risk, or safety owner.
  • Unit assignment, trailer number, and owner note for conditional evidence.
  • Customer or shipper-specific proof requirements.

Exception Path

Missing proof creates review. A blocked state is used when evidence affects safety, claims, customer release, cargo security, or settlement readiness. The owner must record the correction or approved release condition.

Operating consequencePre-trip status must flow into Dispatch, Safety, Operations Record, and Settlements before a load is treated as clean.Revision note: v2.4 adds cargo securement photo and conditional release owner.
Dispatch / v2.1 / Effective May 30, 2026

HOS Escalation Procedure

Purpose: make HOS, ELD, and ready-again timing visible before dispatch commits a driver, releases a load, or delays a customer-facing movement.

Owner
Dispatch manager
Approver
Safety lead
Review cadence
Weekly exceptions
Applies to
Loads affected by drive-time limits or ELD status

Operating Standard

Loads with insufficient available HOS for the plan must be held, reassigned, or conditionally staged only when the next legal-ready window and owner decision are visible.

Required Evidence

  • Available HOS time and next ready-again timestamp or condition.
  • ELD sync status and last verified update.
  • Route impact, delivery risk, and customer communication owner.
  • Named dispatcher and safety reviewer when a release decision is conditional.

Exception Path

When HOS blocks release, the record must show whether dispatch waits for reset, reassigns the driver, splits the work, cancels release, or holds pending safety approval.

Operating consequenceDriver capability, release status, route plan, and customer update must match the HOS clearance state.Revision note: v2.1 adds ready-again countdown and reassignment path.
Claims / v1.8 / Effective May 20, 2026

Cargo and Seal Verification

Purpose: preserve cargo, seal, and securement evidence before the fleet loses the ability to explain a shortage, damage claim, rejected delivery, or settlement dispute.

Owner
Operations lead
Approver
Claims owner
Review cadence
Per exception plus monthly trend
Applies to
Loads with cargo, seal, securement, or claims exposure

Operating Standard

Loads requiring seal, cargo, or securement evidence must retain proof before release and at delivery when customer, claims, or billing review depends on that evidence.

Required Evidence

  • Seal number, seal photo, or written exception if seal is not applicable.
  • Cargo condition and securement proof for bracing, restraint, blocking, or special handling.
  • Receiver exception note, shortage notation, or damage photo when condition changes.
  • Owner note tying proof to load, unit, driver, customer, and settlement packet.

Exception Path

If seal or cargo proof is missing, the load enters review until operations records whether dispatch, claims, customer service, or settlement is affected and names the next follow-up owner.

Operating consequenceClaims exposure and settlement readiness must show whether cargo/seal proof supports or blocks the operating decision.Revision note: v1.8 adds securement proof and receiver exception linkage.
Safety / v2.0 / Effective Apr 28, 2026

Claims and Incident Response

Purpose: create one record of incident facts, owner actions, evidence, dispatch impact, and settlement consequence before details scatter across calls and emails.

Owner
Safety lead
Approver
Operations lead
Review cadence
Per incident plus closeout review
Applies to
Accidents, inspections, cargo claims, and customer complaints

Operating Standard

Incident records must capture what happened, affected load, driver, unit, photos, statements, customer notice, insurance notice when applicable, and the dispatch or settlement consequence.

Required Evidence

  • Event summary, date/time, location, load, driver, unit, and customer.
  • Photos, statements, police/inspection notes, claim notice, or customer complaint record.
  • Corrective action, coaching, retraining, repair, or return-to-dispatch condition.
  • Billing, claim, or settlement hold status if money or proof is affected.

Exception Path

A claim or incident remains under review until the safety owner records the clearance action, affected records, operating consequence, and whether dispatch or settlement remains held.

Operating consequenceIncident status must be visible in Safety, Operations Record, and any affected settlement packet.Revision note: v2.0 adds closeout evidence and settlement impact fields.
Billing / v3.0 / Effective Jun 10, 2026

Billing Packet Requirements

Purpose: define the proof needed before revenue moves from delivery completion to invoice, factoring review, customer payment, or settlement support.

Owner
Billing owner
Approver
Finance lead
Review cadence
Monthly aging and deduction review
Applies to
Invoices, factoring packets, proof packets, and customer payment follow-up

Operating Standard

A billing packet may be marked complete only when rate confirmation, invoice draft, signed BOL/POD, delivery proof, accessorial support, and exception notes are present or formally waived.

Required Evidence

  • Rate confirmation, invoice draft, customer reference, and load identifier.
  • Signed BOL, POD, receiver evidence, and delivery exception notes.
  • Lumper, detention, TONU, accessorial, claim, or deduction support.
  • Factoring or payment-path requirements when applicable.

Exception Path

Missing proof creates a billing hold with owner, amount affected when known, proof needed, next follow-up date, and whether driver pay or settlement is also affected.

Operating consequencePacket readiness must flow into Settlements, AR aging, customer follow-up, and finance visibility.Revision note: v3.0 adds factoring/payment-path and deduction support fields.
Records / v1.6 / Effective Mar 31, 2026

Document Retention Standard

Purpose: make records usable by attaching each file to the operating decision it supports, rather than storing disconnected uploads.

Owner
Business operations
Approver
Governance owner
Review cadence
Semiannual sample audit
Applies to
Driver, dispatch, safety, billing, HR, governance, and proof records

Operating Standard

Records must be retained with the operating item they support, including document type, version, owner, effective date, source, and relationship to driver, load, unit, customer, or settlement record.

Required Evidence

  • Document type, source, upload/creation date, and record owner.
  • Review state, effective date, version, and superseded record link when applicable.
  • Connected driver, load, proof, safety, billing, HR, or governance record.
  • Retention exception note for missing, unreadable, stale, duplicate, or replaced files.

Exception Path

Unreadable, duplicate, missing, or stale documents remain in review until business operations resolves the file, records a retention exception, or links a superseding record.

Operating consequenceRecord quality must affect readiness, billing, safety review, or governance state when the document controls work.Revision note: v1.6 adds superseded-record and retention-exception handling.
Governance / v1.9 / Effective Jul 8, 2026

Access and Data Governance

Purpose: protect sensitive operating records by tying role access, owner approval, and data changes to visible governance evidence.

Owner
Governance owner
Approver
Executive sponsor
Review cadence
Monthly access review plus offboarding event
Applies to
Users touching driver, payroll, safety, billing, customer, or governance records

Operating Standard

Users should receive the minimum role access needed for their work. Sensitive driver, payroll, safety, billing, customer, and governance records require owner-aware access and visible change history.

Required Evidence

  • User role, business reason, approving owner, and access start/change/removal date.
  • Role authority for create, view, edit, approve, export, and delete actions.
  • Audit note for sensitive record changes or exception access.
  • Offboarding or role-change confirmation when access should end.

Exception Path

Access exceptions remain under governance review until the owner approves, limits, or removes access and records the operating reason, time limit, and affected records.

Operating consequenceRole authority must determine who can alter readiness, safety, settlement, finance, and governance records.Revision note: v1.9 adds offboarding confirmation and export/delete authority checks.

Governance workflow

A written rule becomes useful only after assignment, acknowledgment, and audit evidence are visible.

  1. 1Drafted

    Owner creates or updates rule.

  2. 2Reviewed

    Functional lead checks operating fit.

  3. 3Approved

    Decision owner records approval.

  4. 4Published

    Current version replaces old copy.

  5. 5Assigned

    Workers or roles receive the rule.

  6. 6Acknowledged

    Completion state is tracked.

  7. 7Audited

    Evidence and exceptions are reviewed.

  8. 8Revised

    Updates return to the library.

Operational consequence

Procedure gaps should produce assigned work, not vague risk.

When a policy is due, blocked, or missing acknowledgment, the next owner is clear. Driver readiness, dispatch release, safety review, settlement billing, and business operations can all show the rule that controls the decision.

This keeps policy management close to operating work. A driver-file policy can affect readiness, a billing-packet rule can affect invoice preparation, and an access-governance rule can affect who may update sensitive records. The library gives each consequence a path back to the page where the work is happening.
