Governance workflow

A written rule becomes useful only after assignment, acknowledgment, and audit evidence are visible.

  1. 1Drafted

    Owner creates or updates rule.

  2. 2Reviewed

    Functional lead checks operating fit.

  3. 3Approved

    Decision owner records approval.

  4. 4Published

    Current version replaces old copy.

  5. 5Assigned

    Workers or roles receive the rule.

  6. 6Acknowledged

    Completion state is tracked.

  7. 7Audited

    Evidence and exceptions are reviewed.

  8. 8Revised

    Updates return to the library.

Operational consequence

Procedure gaps should produce assigned work, not vague risk.

When a policy is due, blocked, or missing acknowledgment, the next owner is clear. Driver readiness, dispatch release, safety review, settlement billing, and business operations can all show the rule that controls the decision.

This keeps policy management close to operating work. A driver-file policy can affect readiness, a billing-packet rule can affect invoice preparation, and an access-governance rule can affect who may update sensitive records. The library gives each consequence a path back to the page where the work is happening.

Evidence record

Use operating records to show which rule affected a release, hold, or review decision.

Office owner

Route overdue acknowledgment or version review back to the business owner responsible for follow-up.

Governance trace

Show the approval state, evidence link, and record owner when a policy change affects operating work.
