Load Readiness

Control the load from intake through proof, billing, and settlement.

BOF does not need to act as the dispatcher to make the load easier to govern. It organizes the readiness gates, documents, exceptions, owners, and proof packet that determine whether the work can move cleanly.

Lifecycle

Pre-trip -> In-route -> Delivery/POD -> Post-delivery

Each stage keeps the record, required proof, owner, exception, and next action visible.

Lifecycle overview

One load, four control stages.

01

Pre-trip

Customer/load intake, driver and equipment assignment, readiness review, and required proof standards are set before release.

02

In-route

Exceptions, detention, seal issues, appointment changes, and document gaps are assigned while the load is active.

03

Delivery/POD

POD, receiver notes, photos, signatures, and delivery exception evidence are tied to the load record.

04

Post-delivery

Proof packet assembly, billing readiness, settlement release, claims, and exception follow-up move from the same record.

Proof assets

The load is not complete until the proof is inspectable.

BOF keeps the proof list concrete so billing, settlement, and claims do not depend on memory or scattered messages.

Load control flow

Where BOF helps the record stay usable.

Customer/load intake

Capture lane, appointment, commodity, proof expectations, rate context, accessorial risk, and customer requirements.

Driver and equipment assignment

Connect assignment to driver readiness, unit availability, HOS pressure, equipment fit, and special handling notes.

Pre-trip readiness

Review documents, dispatch release conditions, safety notes, and owner signoff before the load moves.

In-route exception handling

Record delay, detention, seal, damage, customer change, or missing-proof issues with owner and consequence.

Delivery and POD

Attach POD, receiver name, timestamps, dock/cargo photos, exception notes, and required follow-up.

Proof packet assembly

Group BOL, POD, receipts, photos, claim notes, and accessorial support into a usable billing packet.

Billing readiness

Confirm invoice support, customer proof rules, claim impact, and missing item owner.

Settlement release

Make pay-affecting proof, deductions, reimbursements, holds, and approvals visible before release.
