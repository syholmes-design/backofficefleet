Cash moves faster when dispatch, proof, and billing share the same readiness view.

Alignment lens

Does finance know what operations already knows?

The joined record makes load readiness, proof, pay, and billing state visible to both teams.

Dispatch

Release context

Load status, driver assignment, unit gate, appointment, accessorial notes, and exception state.

Safety

Hold consequence

Driver credential, HOS, incident, inspection, or policy issue that affects settlement release.

Billing

Invoice readiness

Proof, rate terms, customer reference, factoring path, and claim or dispute status.

Payroll

Pay basis

Driver classification, rate basis, deductions, reimbursements, detention, and settlement statement logic.

Alignment route

  1. Join: connect dispatch record, driver, proof packet, billing state, and pay profile.
  2. Compare: surface differences between operational release and finance release.
  3. Resolve: assign proof, billing, pay, or safety questions to the right owner.
  4. Release: keep the final pay and invoice context tied to the load record.
