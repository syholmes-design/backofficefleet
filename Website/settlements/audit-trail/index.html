The record should show why money was released, held, changed, or escalated.

Audit lens

Can the team explain the settlement without hunting through spreadsheets, emails, and folders?

Audit readiness means the payment decision carries the evidence and review note that supported it.

History

Status sequence

Ready, review, held, blocked, released, disputed, or paid states with timestamp and owner.

Calculation

Pay logic

Rate basis, deductions, reimbursements, detention, accessorials, and approval notes.

Evidence

Packet trail

Proof documents, photos, BOL, POD, receipts, claims, and customer confirmations.

Control

Review note

Who cleared the issue, what changed, and whether the release affected cash-flow visibility.

Audit trail route

  1. Log: record the settlement status, issue, and cash impact.
  2. Attach: preserve proof and pay calculation evidence.
  3. Review: capture the owner, decision, and clearance note.
  4. Retain: keep the final packet state available for management review.
