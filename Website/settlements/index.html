Packet anatomy

Settlement readiness depends on proof, billing support, driver review, and cash-flow context.

Each packet item below is demo content. It shows what the record is, who usually provides it, why the fleet needs it, and what happens when it is missing before settlement or billing release.

Packet itemWhat it isWho provides itWhy it mattersIf missing
Proof PacketPOD, BOL, dock photos, seal/cargo proof, receiver notes, and closeout evidence.Driver, dispatcher, customer, receiver, or packet owner.Confirms the work was completed and supports invoice/settlement review.Settlement and invoice readiness move to hold or review.
Billing PacketInvoice support, rate confirmation, POD, accessorial approval, and customer billing notes.Customer, dispatcher, billing owner, and document reviewer.Shows why the invoice is ready and which records support charges.Billing cannot confidently release; receivables follow-up may wait.
Driver Settlement VerificationPay basis, miles, stop pay, bonus, reimbursement, deduction, and reviewer note.Operations, payroll coordinator, contractor settlement owner, or finance reviewer.Connects pay lines to trip records without pretending BOF is payroll.Pay stays in review until the fleet's proper reviewer confirms support.

Detention / AccessorialsApproved waiting time, extra stop, layover, toll, or other supported charge.Dispatcher, customer, driver, or billing owner.Shows why an extra charge or pay line belongs in the packet.Accessorial pay or invoice lines remain under review.
