BOF Aggregator Partner Offer

Turn fleet relationships into a readiness-checked carrier network.

BOF helps aggregator partners turn their fleet relationships into a readiness-checked carrier network, without requiring the aggregator to manage daily dispatch, compliance chasing, or proof packet assembly.

Discuss an aggregator pilot Open the guided aggregator demo
Partner fit For organizations that already reach many fleets.

Trucking associations, factoring companies, insurance brokers, ELD and telematics vendors, fleet networks, compliance consultants, and service providers can use BOF as an operating layer for readiness, onboarding, proof, and exception workflows.

Aggregator problem

Many fleet relationships do not automatically become usable capacity.

Aggregator partners often know many fleets, but the operational question is narrower: which fleets are ready, compliant, document-complete, and usable for a specific customer or network opportunity?

Availability gap

Available capacity is not the same as usable capacity.

A carrier may be in the network while its driver files, insurance, equipment records, proof requirements, or customer approvals still need review.

Reputation risk

Referring an unready carrier can damage trust.

When a fleet is not ready, the aggregator can absorb the relationship damage even if the issue was documentation, proof, or administrative follow-up.

Scale pressure

Manual follow-up is hard to scale.

Compliance chasing, document collection, proof packet organization, and exception follow-up become difficult when the network grows.

Role boundary

Aggregators may want network value without operating every detail.

BOF gives partners a way to support network readiness without turning the partner into a day-to-day dispatcher or compliance manager.

Operating clarity

The network needs status before action.

Ready, needs review, blocked, and eligible-with-conditions records should be visible before a customer-facing workflow depends on them.

Proof burden

Proof rules have to follow the record.

Customer proof, POD, seal, lumper, claims, and settlement documents need to stay attached to the carrier, driver, customer, and equipment combination.

What BOF does

BOF surfaces readiness fit, rules, proof, and exceptions.

BOF supports intake, readiness assessment, document organization, onboarding profiles, proof packet organization, and exception visibility. It helps separate usable capacity from review-needed or blocked capacity without creating a freight marketplace.

Readiness intake

BOF runs intake and readiness assessment.

Fleet, carrier, driver, equipment, customer, and proof requirements are captured as reviewable operating records.

Rule profile

BOF organizes carrier, driver, equipment, customer, and proof requirements.

Rules stay connected to the record so eligibility is not treated as a vague network label.

Classification

BOF classifies fleets into clear operating states.

Ready, needs review, blocked, and eligible-with-conditions statuses make the next action visible.

Workflow support

BOF supports document intake, onboarding, proof packets, and exceptions.

Partners see where administrative follow-up is needed before the issue becomes reputational or financial friction.

Member and customer fleet value

BOF helps member fleets clean up the back-office work behind readiness.

For participating fleets, the offer is practical: fewer scattered records, clearer document status, better readiness visibility, and more organized proof for settlement, claims, and customer follow-up.

Onboarding

Fleet onboarding support

BOF helps create onboarding profiles for fleets, drivers, equipment, and proof requirements.

Driver files

Driver document intake and readiness tracking

CDL, medical card, MVR, DQF, training, and restriction follow-up can be tracked as readiness evidence.

Compliance

DQF and compliance readiness support

BOF helps organize the administrative side of compliance review without replacing licensed or responsible-party judgment.

Settlement

Settlement and proof packet organization

PODs, BOLs, seal photos, lumper receipts, and supporting proof can stay connected to the operating record.

Claims

Claims and proof records

Visibility

Reduced administrative friction

Fleets get clearer status on what is cleared, what is blocked, and what needs follow-up.

Commercial models

Four ways an aggregator partner can use BOF.

BOF's aggregator model is based on helping partners convert fleet relationships into a structured readiness network. The partner pays BOF for assessment, implementation, onboarding support, and ongoing readiness visibility. Referred fleets may later become BOF customers, but the core model is not a referral-fee or revenue-share program.
