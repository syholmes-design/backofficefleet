Who this is for

Aggregator-facing groups that need a practical operating layer.

BOF is useful when an organization already supports many fleets, carriers, or contractors and needs clearer readiness, documents, proof, compliance follow-through, and back-office coordination.

Associations

Trucking associations

Use BOF to give members a readiness and document-control path without building member software from scratch.

Operators

Dispatch and back-office operators

Use BOF to organize customer intake, driver checks, document follow-up, proof collection, and settlement-support work.

Finance

Factoring companies

Use BOF to help fleets prepare cleaner proof packets, billing support records, and funding-ready documentation.

Risk

Transportation insurance brokers

Services
