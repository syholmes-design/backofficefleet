Fleet integration plan

How BOF onboards a fleet into a repeatable operating workflow.

Step 1

Fleet intake

Company profile, MC/DOT identity, truck count, driver count, current systems, payroll/accounting setup, and compliance pain points.

Step 2

Driver roster import

Driver list, CDL/medical/MVR status, employment status, owner-operator versus company driver, and missing documents.

Step 3

Document readiness review

DQF, CDL, medical card, MVR, insurance, W-9, equipment docs, and customer approval requirements.

Step 4

Workflow configuration

Payroll, settlements, customer portal, document intake, proof packet requirements, and exception handling.

Step 5

Pilot operating week

Test load flow, proof packet, settlement packet, missing document escalation, and customer portal visibility.

Step 6

30-day stabilization

Weekly reporting, readiness score, open exceptions, settlement blockers, document gaps, and fleet-owner review.

Aggregator integration model

Support aggregator relationships without implying brokerage or dispatch.

BOF supports aggregator relationships by standardizing fleet readiness, documentation, administrative workflows, and operating visibility across referred fleets.

Careful operating position

BOF does not need to publicly disclose every fleet relationship to every aggregator. BOF can help aggregators understand which fleets are administratively ready, document-ready, and operationally supportable.

BOF should avoid language that suggests it controls dispatch, books freight, or acts as a broker unless that legal model is later approved.

Constituent website strategy

One platform website with three audience-specific pathways.

Rather than launching three separate websites immediately, BOF should maintain one platform site with clearly separated constituent journeys. Dedicated landing paths can serve fleet operators, aggregator partners, and drivers without fragmenting the brand or multiplying maintenance work.

Path 1

Fleet Operators

Lead with administrative operating support, driver readiness, document control, settlement visibility, customer portal workflows, and proof-driven operating discipline.

Path 2

Aggregators / Network Partners

Show carrier readiness, network visibility, document standards, proof collection, and back-office workflow support without implying dispatch, brokerage, or transportation control.

Path 3

Drivers / BOF Vault

Explain document intake, DQF organization, credential readiness, proof packet support, and driver-facing record clarity as a BOF Vault journey.

Why one site first:
  • One site is easier to maintain during MVP.
  • Unified BOF messaging reduces confusion for early investors, fleets, aggregators, and drivers.
  • Shared demos and proof assets can support multiple audiences without duplicating production work.
  • Dedicated landing paths still allow each audience to self-select into the right demo path.
