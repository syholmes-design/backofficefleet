BOF Fleet Operator Offer

Reduce back-office drag before it becomes operational risk.

BOF helps fleet operators organize driver readiness, document workflows, proof packets, settlements, claims, onboarding, and back-office exceptions so small and mid-size fleets can operate with more control and less administrative drag.

Fleet fit For fleets where the back office is carrying too much by memory.

BOF is built for 20-75 truck fleets, small and mid-size for-hire carriers, and operators facing driver readiness, settlements, proof packets, onboarding, claims, and document-follow-up pressure.

Who this is for

Fleet operators that need structure before another disconnected software subscription.

BOF is for small and mid-size fleets that already have phones, email, folders, portals, spreadsheets, and a TMS, but still lose time chasing the same driver, document, proof, and settlement details.

Fleet size

20-75 truck operators

Fleets large enough to feel administrative drag, but small enough that dropped follow-up still lands on the owner or operations lead.

Carrier type

For-hire trucking carriers

Operators that need clearer readiness, proof, and back-office follow-through before a record becomes a delay or dispute.

Operating pressure

Admin-heavy fleets

Teams carrying driver files, DQF work, claims records, POD support, and settlement packets across too many disconnected places.

The problem BOF solves

Back-office issues become operational delays when nobody can see the whole record.

Fleet owners lose time chasing documents, expirations, proof, settlements, claims, and exceptions. Driver readiness is often tracked manually, while DQFs, CDL records, medical cards, MVRs, onboarding packets, claims records, PODs, and settlement support stay scattered across systems and inboxes.

Document drag

Records are scattered when the fleet needs a clear answer.

CDLs, med cards, MVRs, DQFs, onboarding packets, PODs, and claims records need a better operating structure.

Readiness risk

Driver readiness should not depend on memory.

Manual tracking makes it harder to know what is current, what needs review, and what could block the next workflow.

Owner burden

The same follow-up keeps returning to leadership.

Fleet owners and managers get pulled into document chasing when the back office does not have clear status, ownership, and next action.

Proof gaps

Proof packets need to stay tied to the operating record.

PODs, BOLs, seal photos, lumper receipts, detention records, claim evidence, and settlement support need to remain usable after the trip.

Onboarding drag

New driver records can start incomplete.

Onboarding profile creation needs structure around documents, status, missing pieces, and follow-up responsibility.

Exception pressure

Small back-office misses can become operational problems.

BOF helps surface readiness, proof, and exception status before the issue becomes harder to unwind.

What BOF does

BOF helps fleets organize readiness, proof, and back-office workflows before exceptions become operational problems.

BOF supports the administrative work behind driver readiness, document intake, compliance readiness tracking, proof packet organization, settlement support, claims records, onboarding profiles, exception visibility, and back-office workflow follow-through.

Driver readiness

Driver records become easier to review.

BOF helps organize readiness status, document categories, expiration needs, and review-needed records.

Document intake

Documents move into clearer categories.

CDL, medical card, MVR, DQF, onboarding, proof, claims, and settlement documents become easier to classify and inspect.

Proof packets

Proof stays connected to the workflow.

POD, BOL, delivery photos, seal verification, lumper receipts, detention records, and customer confirmation can be organized as supporting evidence.

Settlement and claims

Post-trip support is easier to follow.

BOF helps organize the records that support settlement packets, factoring readiness, driver settlement review, claims response, and exception follow-up.

Onboarding

Driver and fleet profiles start with better structure.

BOF helps create onboarding profiles that show what is present, what is missing, and who owns the next administrative action.

Exception visibility

Owners see what is cleared, blocked, or waiting.

Back-office issues become visible as operational states instead of loose notes spread across people and tools.
