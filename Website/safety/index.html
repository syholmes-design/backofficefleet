Safety operating program

Safety should explain what happened, what it affects, and what clears the driver or load.

This demo program treats safety as an operating control layer. It does not promise legal compliance; it shows how a fleet can keep qualification records, telematics events, pre-trip evidence, incident response, and corrective action connected to dispatch and settlement decisions.
