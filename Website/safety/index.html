Evidence and Policy Control

The page shows what happened, what it affects, and what clears the driver or load.

HOS and telematics inputs

HOS availability, ELD sync state, harsh braking, speed, following distance, and seat belt indicators can inform review status when available.

Corrective action

Driver coaching, owner verification, retraining, proof upload, and return-to-dispatch status stay linked to the safety queue.

Policy acknowledgment and training

Safety policies, training acknowledgments, and version history remain visible beside the record they control.

Pre-trip and equipment evidence

Photos, cargo securement, tire checks, seal evidence, and unit assignment context can support dispatch release review.
