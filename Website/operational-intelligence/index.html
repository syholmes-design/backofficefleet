Operational Intelligence

Operational Intelligence Command Center

BOF converts operational data into actionable readiness and operational intelligence during the trip.

BOF provides visibility across fleets, carriers, drivers, assets, and operational workflows by integrating information from telematics, RFID, driver systems, documentation systems, and operating records. BOF consumes these signals; it does not replace telematics systems, RFID platforms, dispatch services, freight brokerage, transportation arrangement, or motor carrier operations.
