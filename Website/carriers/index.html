Registry rules

Carrier eligibility should include DOT/MC authority, insurance limits, cargo coverage, W-9, agreement, ACH/payment setup, customer approval, and lane or equipment rules.

Reason before release

A review or blocked carrier should say exactly what failed: expired certificate, missing agreement, customer approval gap, low coverage, driver credential issue, or equipment mismatch.

Clearance path

Every exception needs an owner, due date, required proof, affected load, and release consequence so dispatch does not guess.

Protected data

Tax IDs, bank details, commercial terms, and private notes stay protected while the operational readiness answer remains visible.
