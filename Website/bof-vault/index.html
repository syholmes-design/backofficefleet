BOF Vault

Turn document intake into readiness decisions.

BOF Vault is the driver-facing and operations-facing document layer. It receives files, classifies them, applies readiness rules, shows what is missing or blocked, and routes the next owner action before dispatch or settlement gets stuck.

Open BOF Vault Intake Workbench See the readiness engine

What the Vault controls

Every document should answer whether the work can move.

Intake

Collect the file once.

Receive uploads from drivers, dispatch, carriers, customers, or office staff without losing the operating context.

Classify

Attach it to the right record.

Identify whether the document belongs to a driver file, carrier packet, equipment record, load proof, claim, or billing packet.

Decide

Show the blocker and clearance path.

Mark the item ready, review, missing, expired, blocked, or cleared with a named owner and next action.
