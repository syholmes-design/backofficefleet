**Subject:** CDLIS employer verification for a service provider supporting multiple motor carriers

Dear AAMVA Customer Service,

My name is Sylvester Holmes, and I'm the founder of BackOfficeFleet, a Nathson company. We provide outsourced back-office and compliance services to transportation aggregators and their affiliated motor carriers. This includes maintaining driver qualification files and supporting the motor vehicle record checks those carriers must perform at hire and each year.

We understand that systems such as State-to-State and PDPS exist for exchange between jurisdictions and are not available to private parties. We are not seeking access to them. We are interested in the products AAMVA offers to the private sector, in particular **CDLIS Third Party Access for CDLIS Employers Verification**, and possibly **Driver License Data Verification (DLDV)**.

Could you help us understand the following, or direct us to the right team:

1. **Eligibility:** can a service provider act on behalf of multiple motor carriers, as each carrier's authorized agent, under one AAMVA arrangement? Or must each carrier enroll on its own, or go through an existing approved provider?
2. **Enrollment:** what agreements, application materials, security reviews or audits are required? Is a non-disclosure agreement needed before pricing is shared?
3. **Consent:** what driver consent and recordkeeping requirements apply, and how does AAMVA audit them?
4. **Technical access:** how do users connect (for example web services), and is a test environment available?
5. **Fees:** we understand user fees have been suspended since October 2023. Are there plans and timing for reinstating them for private users?
6. **Continuity:** does current litigation over the federal request for CDLIS records affect the availability of the employer verification product?
7. **DLDV:** is DLDV available to a service provider on the same basis, and how many jurisdictions participate?
8. **Jurisdiction relationships:** we also plan to work directly with state records programs. Would AAMVA's regional staff, or a group such as the Third Party Agents or External Access working groups, be the right place to learn how jurisdictions approach service providers?

We are not requesting any data at this time. We would welcome a short call.

Thank you for your time.
