## Page-Specific Review

### Dispatch

The content is framed as release-control workflow: requirement identified, method selected, instructions sent, proof uploaded, exception resolved, evidence retained. It is labeled as illustrative operating data.

### Safety

The content uses `supports cargo-securement oversight` and explicitly avoids saying Freight Brace ensures compliance. The image is tied only to cargo securement and safety evidence, not unrelated credential holds.

### Drivers

The content focuses on driver clarity: instructions, equipment confirmation, photographs, acknowledgment, issue reporting, and release confirmation. It does not promise 24/7 support or guaranteed damage avoidance.

### Settlements

The content treats Freight Brace as supporting evidence that stays with a load. It explicitly does not claim guaranteed payment or automatic prevention of settlement holds.
