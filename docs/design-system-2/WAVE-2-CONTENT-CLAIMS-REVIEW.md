# BOF Design System 2.0 Wave 2 Content Claims Review

## Claims Position

Wave 2 presents an illustrative public product experience using synthetic data. The pages avoid claims of live private document storage, automatic verification, guaranteed readiness, guaranteed compliance, live chat, live notifications, production integrations, government approval, or legal advice.

## Route Review

Documents:
- Uses document states only when the illustrative record supports them: reviewed, classified, approved, issue open, and ready for release.
- Explains review, rejection, renewal, version history, access, and audit trail without implying uploaded documents are automatically verified.

Policies & Procedures:
- Uses support language: supports policy governance, supports compliance oversight, and preserves evidence of acknowledgment and review.
- States that BOF does not provide legal advice or replace professional compliance judgment.
- Keeps superseded and archived policies as retained evidence, not deleted records.

BOF Vault:
- Labels the page as an illustrative product experience.
- Uses planned secure workflow language where appropriate.
- Uses Open Messages instead of Start Live Chat.
- States that no files are submitted or stored from the public page.
