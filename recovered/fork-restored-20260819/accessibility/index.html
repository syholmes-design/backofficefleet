Accessibility

Operational information should remain understandable and reachable.

BOF is working to provide clear structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, alternative text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior across the public site. This page describes the current intent and feedback path without claiming a formal conformance level.

Request accessibility support →

Keyboard and focus

Public navigation and interactive controls are intended to work without a mouse and show a visible focus indicator.

Readable structure

Pages use headings, landmarks, labels, text alternatives, and responsive reading order so content remains understandable without imagery.

Motion and media

Reduced-motion preferences are respected where implemented; audio, video, and complex demo surfaces may still have limitations that require assistance.

Support path

Tell BOF the page, task, and barrier you encountered.

Useful feedback identifies the URL, device or assistive technology, intended task, and what prevented completion. Do not include sensitive personal or fleet records.

  1. 01

    Name the page or demo route

    Include the URL and the label of the control, section, table, document, or media item involved.

  2. 02

    Describe the intended task

    Explain what you were trying to read, reach, activate, submit, or understand.

  3. 03

    Describe the barrier

    Share the keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, zoom, motion, caption, touch, or layout problem.

  4. 04

    Request an alternative

    Ask for the information or next step in a format that works for the immediate need.

Qualification

Current accessibility posture

BOF does not claim a formal WCAG conformance level on this page.

  • The site is periodically reviewed for keyboard access, focus visibility, contrast, headings, alternatives, responsive behavior, and reduced motion.
  • Dense product demonstrations, generated documents, media, and third-party/browser behavior can introduce limitations.
  • BOF will use reported barriers to prioritize practical remediation and an accessible alternative where feasible.
  • Accessibility feedback should not include medical details, credentials, driver records, or other sensitive information.
