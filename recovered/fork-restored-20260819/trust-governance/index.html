Governance summary

An illustrative control map for the operating surfaces buyers inspect.

This static demonstration organizes proposed control areas around the questions a fleet operator would naturally ask: who should access records, how AI should be governed, how driver data should be handled, and what should happen when systems or vendors need review.

Illustrative governance framework. These summaries are discussion prompts, not full policy artifacts, certifications, implementation claims, effective-date assertions, or proof that customer workspaces currently contain these documents.
AI

AI governance

AI-assisted review is positioned as support for human operating decisions, not a replacement for judgment.

DC

Data classification

Driver files, BOL images, carrier packets, and operational records are handled according to classification and protection expectations.

AC

Access control

Acceptable use, authentication, remote access, and communications policies support controlled system use.

VA

Vendor access

Third-party tools, ELD providers, TMS connections, and vendor systems are framed as controlled access points.

ELD

ELD and mobile security

Mobile uploads, driver records, ELD-related data, and field document capture sit inside device and access controls.

IR

Incident response

Security and operational incidents are summarized for buyer confidence and reviewed in controlled settings when appropriate.

BC

Continuity

Recovery and continuity planning supports the idea that BOF is an operating layer, not a loose collection of documents.

RT

Review trail

Policy references connect back to the release packets, readiness records, and dispatch decisions in the partner workflow.
