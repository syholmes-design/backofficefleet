Qualification

Administrative support stays inside a defined boundary.

Resources are educational, use qualified examples, and do not provide legal, regulatory, safety, accounting, tax, or financial advice.

  • Public screens, records, statuses, and integration examples are static or synthetic unless a page explicitly states an authorized live capability.
  • The fleet, carrier, employer, agency, customer, and qualified professionals retain their operating, legal, safety, regulatory, and financial authority.
  • BOF does not guarantee release, acceptance, compliance, payment, collection, savings, audit, safety, or financial outcomes.
  • A working session qualifies the record set, responsible owner, service boundary, intended output, and appropriate next action.
