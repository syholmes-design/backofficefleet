Protected Network Relationships

BOF supports the aggregator's network without taking over the relationship.

Aggregators own their carrier relationships. BOF helps organize readiness, documentation, compliance, claims, settlements, and administrative workflows while preserving participant boundaries.

Relationship ownership stays with the aggregator.

BOF does not need to expose private BOF customer relationships to create value. The command center focuses on readiness, documentation, status, evidence, and next-action visibility.

Capacity visibility stays administrative.

BOF can support capacity visibility and readiness while preserving participant boundaries. The demo does not create freight placement, freight matching, load-board, dispatch, or brokerage functionality.

Operating guardrail

BOF supports readiness, visibility, documentation, claims coordination, settlements, business operations, and administrative workflows. BOF does not act as a broker, dispatcher, freight marketplace, or motor carrier in this demo workflow.
