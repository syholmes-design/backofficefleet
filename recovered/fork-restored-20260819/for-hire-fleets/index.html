Qualification

Built to support the carrier's operation

BOF organizes operating evidence and follow-through. The fleet retains the authority and professional responsibilities attached to the work.

  • BOF does not act as the motor carrier, freight broker, dispatcher of record, safety authority, insurer, factoring company, or payment processor.
  • Public screens and integration pages are static simulations unless an authorized agreement explicitly states a live capability.
  • BOF does not guarantee dispatch release, on-time performance, billing acceptance, collection, settlement outcome, or claim resolution.
  • A working session qualifies the record set, operating owner, service boundary, and responsible next step before any engagement.
