Problem

Internal fleet records often live across too many owners.

Driver files may sit with HR, vehicle records with operations, incident notes with safety, completion proof with field managers, and cost follow-up with finance. BOF helps private fleets see what is ready, what is blocked, and who owns the next action.

Scattered records

Proof gaps

Handoff friction

30-day pilot

Private Fleet Pilot: 30-Day Readiness and Proof Workflow Assessment

A focused review that shows where private fleet readiness, proof, and handoff work is clear, blocked, or at risk.

Pilot steps

  1. Map fleet type, driver roster, vehicle/equipment records, and current workflows.
  2. Review driver readiness and core document categories.
  3. Review proof, photo, and completion records.
  4. Identify internal handoff gaps and exception points.
  5. Build BOF readiness profiles for selected drivers and equipment.
  6. Produce a private fleet readiness and proof workflow report.
  7. Recommend pilot scope and operating support model.

Deliverables

  • Driver readiness matrix
  • Vehicle/equipment readiness summary
  • Document gap summary
  • Proof/completion workflow map
  • Exception inventory
  • Recommended BOF pilot scope
