Business office lifecycle

Dispatch, safety, and settlement records feed HR and Finance work.

Business Operations is where the operating record becomes back-office follow-through: HR readiness, payroll administration, customer billing, accounts receivable, document management, and reporting. BOF helps organize the work without acting as a payroll provider, accounting firm, tax advisor, lender, bank, factoring company, or legal authority.

HR Tier

Human resources, onboarding, training, benefits coordination, worker records, and driver readiness stay visible as operating work.

Accounting & Finance

Billing, payables, receivables, payroll coordination, settlement support, and reporting use the same record context.

Finance Tier

Finance follow-through connects invoices, settlement packets, factoring packet support, cash-flow visibility, and review status.

Payroll Administration

BOF organizes payroll inputs and settlement support for review; pay decisions remain with the fleet and proper provider.

Customer Billing

PODs, lumper receipts, delivery proof, accessorials, and claim notes support cleaner billing packet review.

Accounts Receivable

Open receivables stay connected to invoice readiness, proof packet status, customer follow-up, and unresolved holds.

Document Management

Driver, dispatch, safety, settlement, billing, and policy records stay organized as a business reference.

Operational Reporting

Leadership can see where work is ready, under review, blocked, completed, or waiting on an owner.
