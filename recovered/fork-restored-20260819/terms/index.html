Website terms

Use the site as operational information - not as an automated decision authority.

BOF public pages and demonstrations explain an operating approach and simulate product workflows. They do not create a service agreement, replace professional judgment, or prove that a live integration, compliance outcome, or financial result exists.

Static simulations

Demo screens, records, statuses, documents, and workflow results are staged examples unless a page explicitly states otherwise.

No automatic engagement

Browsing, using a demo, or sending an email does not create a contract or authorize BOF to act for a fleet.

Qualified decisions remain human

Carrier, safety, legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, payroll, insurance, dispatch, and financial decisions remain with responsible people and professionals.

Responsible use

Inspect the operating model without treating the demonstration as live authority.

Visitors may use the public site to understand BOF, evaluate fit, and prepare a working-session question. They may not misrepresent staged material, interfere with the site, or rely on it as professional advice.

Qualification

No implied outcomes

Examples clarify a workflow; they are not guarantees.

  • No page guarantees compliance, safety, dispatch release, collection, cash flow, savings, profitability, audit acceptance, or customer approval.
  • No static integration page implies live credentials, API access, synchronization, webhook delivery, or production data exchange.
  • Synthetic documents and examples are not legal, regulatory, payroll, tax, accounting, insurance, or contract templates for unreviewed use.
  • These plain-language terms should receive qualified legal review before being represented as final legal terms.
