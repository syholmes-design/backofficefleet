How BOF works

Start with one operating pressure and make the responsibility visible.

BOF is easiest to evaluate around a real record: a driver file, release question, document packet, settlement exception, carrier review, or owner follow-up queue.

  1. 01

    Name the operating question

    Define the decision the team needs to make and the consequence of leaving it unclear.

  2. 02

    Gather the supporting record

    Connect the documents, dates, people, equipment, and prior actions that support the decision.

  3. 03

    Assign a state and owner

    Show whether the record is ready, watch, or hold and identify the accountable next action.

  4. 04

    Carry the outcome forward

    Keep the decision and evidence available for dispatch, billing, settlement, reporting, or later review.

Qualification

Clear operating boundaries

BOF supports administrative coordination and operating-record discipline. The exact engagement is qualified in a working session.

  • BOF does not replace a motor carrier's safety authority, dispatch authority, or legal responsibilities.
  • BOF does not present its static public demonstrations as live customer data, live integrations, or automated compliance decisions.
  • Legal, tax, accounting, payroll, insurance, and regulatory questions remain with the fleet and its qualified professionals.
  • Claims about services, outcomes, and integrations are limited to what BOF can currently support and demonstrate.
