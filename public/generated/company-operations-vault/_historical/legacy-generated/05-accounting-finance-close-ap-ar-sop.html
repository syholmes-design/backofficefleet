Accounting, Finance Close, AP, and AR SOP
SOP Overview

This Accounting, Finance Close, AP, and AR SOP establishes standardized procedures for month-end closing, accounts payable/receivable management, financial reporting, and billing operations. These procedures ensure accurate financial records, timely payments, and compliance with accounting standards.

Monthly Closing Procedures
  • Revenue Recognition Recognize all earned revenue when services are performed
  • Expense Recording Record all business expenses with proper documentation and approval
  • Bank Reconciliation Reconcile bank accounts daily and resolve discrepancies
  • Accounts Receivable Aging Generate aging report and follow collection procedures
  • Preparation of Financial Statements Prepare balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement
Fuel Reimbursements and Expense Processing

Fuel reimbursements and driver expenses must be processed with proper documentation and approval. All reimbursement requests require receipts, mileage logs, and manager authorization before payment processing.

  • Fuel Receipt Verification Verify fuel receipts against driver mileage and route assignments
  • Reimbursement Calculation Calculate fuel reimbursements based on approved rates and mileage
  • Documentation Requirements Ensure all receipts and supporting documents are attached and legible
Accounts Payable Management
  • Invoice Processing Process vendor invoices within 30 days and maintain accurate records
  • Payment Authorization Require proper authorization for all payments over $1,000
  • Vendor Payment Terms Establish standard payment terms and communicate to all vendors
Financial Reporting
  • Monthly Financial Package Prepare comprehensive monthly financial reports for management review
  • Budget vs Actual Analysis Compare budgeted vs actual performance monthly
  • Variance Reporting Report and explain significant financial variances
Compliance and Audit Procedures
  • Internal Controls Maintain segregation of duties and regular internal audits
  • Documentation Requirements Maintain supporting documentation for all transactions
  • External Audit Coordinate annual external audit and address findings
System Integration
  • Dispatch Integration Connect financial data with dispatch and billing systems
  • Payroll System Interface Ensure seamless data flow between payroll and accounting systems
  • Factoring Integration Link factoring data with receivables management
Quality Control
  • Review Procedures Management review of all financial reports before distribution
  • Approval Authority Establish clear approval matrix for financial transactions
Implementation Notes

Version Control: Maintain SOP version history and update procedures as business needs evolve.

Training Requirements: Ensure finance team is trained on current accounting standards and procedures.

System Integration: Connect accounting procedures with existing BOF financial systems for seamless data flow.
