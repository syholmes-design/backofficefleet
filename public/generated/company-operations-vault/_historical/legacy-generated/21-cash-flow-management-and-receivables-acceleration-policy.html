Purpose

This policy establishes procedures for optimizing cash flow timing, managing receivables, and accelerating invoice processing. This policy covers factoring utilization, billing workflows, proof completion requirements, and cash flow forecasting while maintaining compliance with accounting standards.

Scope

This policy applies to all cash flow management activities, including:

  • Invoice generation and processing
  • Proof of delivery completion
  • Accounts receivable management
  • Factoring and financing arrangements
  • Cash flow forecasting and monitoring
  • Customer payment processing
  • Settlement and payment coordination

Responsibilities

Finance Team

  • Manage cash flow forecasting and monitoring
  • Coordinate factoring and financing activities
  • Oversee accounts receivable management
  • Ensure timely payment processing
  • Maintain cash flow reporting and analysis

Billing Team

  • Generate accurate and timely invoices
  • Ensure proof of delivery completeness
  • Coordinate customer billing requirements
  • Manage invoice exceptions and disputes
  • Maintain billing documentation

Operations Team

  • Ensure proof of delivery completion
  • Provide load completion documentation
  • Coordinate billing information flow
  • Support customer communication
  • Maintain operational records

Cash Flow Maximization Workflow

1. Load Completion Verification

  • Confirm load delivery completion
  • Verify proof of delivery requirements
  • Document any delivery exceptions
  • Obtain customer acceptance confirmation
  • Update load status in system

2. Proof Bundle Completion

  • Collect all required proof documents
  • Verify proof bundle completeness
  • Upload digital proof to system
  • Confirm customer proof requirements
  • Address any proof gaps or issues

3. Invoice Generation

  • Generate invoice within 24 hours of delivery
  • Verify invoice accuracy and completeness
  • Include all required supporting documentation
  • Apply customer billing preferences
  • Submit invoice for approval

4. Factoring Evaluation

  • Evaluate invoice for factoring eligibility
  • Confirm customer credit worthiness
  • Calculate factoring costs and benefits
  • Submit invoice to factoring company
  • Track factoring advance status

5. Payment Processing

  • Monitor customer payment status
  • Follow up on overdue payments
  • Process payment applications
  • Reconcile payment discrepancies
  • Update accounts receivable records

6. Cash Flow Monitoring

  • Update cash flow forecasts daily
  • Monitor cash inflows and outflows
  • Identify cash flow gaps or surpluses
  • Adjust cash management strategies
  • Report cash flow status to management

Receivables Acceleration Procedures

Invoice Processing Acceleration

  • Automate invoice generation where possible
  • Use electronic invoicing systems
  • Implement invoice approval workflows
  • Reduce manual processing steps
  • Monitor processing time metrics

Proof Completion Acceleration

  • Implement digital proof capture systems
  • Use mobile proof upload applications
  • Automate proof verification processes
  • Provide driver proof completion training
  • Monitor proof completion rates

Customer Communication

  • Send invoice notifications promptly
  • Provide clear payment instructions
  • Offer multiple payment options
  • Implement automated payment reminders
  • Maintain customer communication records

Dispute Resolution

  • Establish clear dispute resolution procedures
  • Respond to disputes within 24 hours
  • Document all dispute communications
  • Escalate complex disputes appropriately
  • Track dispute resolution metrics

Factoring Management

Factoring Eligibility Criteria

  • Invoice must be for completed delivery
  • Proof of delivery must be complete
  • Customer must have acceptable credit rating
  • Invoice must not be disputed
  • Customer must be approved by factoring company

Factoring Cost Analysis

  • Calculate factoring fee percentage
  • Determine advance rate percentage
  • Estimate reserve holdback amount
  • Compare factoring vs traditional financing
  • Evaluate cash flow impact

Factoring Process Management

  • Submit eligible invoices promptly
  • Track factoring advance timing
  • Monitor reserve release schedules
  • Reconcile factoring statements
  • Maintain factoring documentation

Factoring Risk Management

  • Monitor customer credit status
  • Track customer payment history
  • Manage recourse factoring exposure
  • Maintain adequate reserve levels
  • Review factoring agreements regularly

Cash Flow Forecasting

Short-term Forecasting (7-30 days)

  • Track expected invoice payments
  • Monitor factoring advance availability
  • Forecast upcoming settlement payments
  • Estimate fuel and operating expenses
  • Calculate net cash position

Medium-term Forecasting (1-3 months)

  • Project seasonal cash flow patterns
  • Estimate customer payment trends
  • Forecast operating expense variations
  • Plan for capital expenditure timing
  • Identify potential cash flow gaps

Long-term Forecasting (3-12 months)

  • Develop annual cash flow projections
  • Plan for financing requirements
  • Evaluate investment opportunities
  • Assess debt service capacity
  • Prepare strategic cash flow plans
