1. Policy Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for payroll processing, compensation administration, and deduction management at Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. It ensures compliance with federal and state regulations while providing clear guidelines for all compensation-related activities managed through the BackOfficeFleet operating system.

This policy applies to all company employees, company drivers, and owner-operators contracted with Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. All payroll activities are organized and managed through BackOfficeFleet to ensure consistency, accuracy, and audit readiness.

2. Compensation Structure
2.1 Company Driver Compensation

Company drivers are compensated based on established pay structures including percentage-of-load rates, mileage rates, hourly rates, or combinations thereof. All compensation rates are documented in individual driver agreements and maintained in BackOfficeFleet.

2.2 Owner-Operator Compensation

Owner-operators receive settlement payments based on load revenue minus applicable deductions and chargebacks. Settlement calculations are processed through BackOfficeFleet with detailed breakdowns provided for each settlement period.

2.3 Percentage-of-Load Pay Structure

For drivers compensated on a percentage-of-load basis, compensation is calculated as a percentage of the gross revenue generated by assigned loads. Percentage rates vary based on experience, equipment type, and route complexity as outlined in individual driver agreements.

Note: Percentage-of-load calculations are automatically processed through BackOfficeFleet to ensure accuracy and consistency across all settlements.
3. Pay Periods and Payment Methods
3.1 Pay Periods

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. processes payroll on a weekly basis. Pay periods run from Monday through Sunday, with payments issued on the following Friday. Owner-operator settlements follow the same weekly schedule.

3.2 Payment Methods

Company employees receive payment via direct deposit. Owner-operators receive settlement payments via direct deposit or check, as specified in their contracts. All payment information is maintained securely in BackOfficeFleet.

3.3 Pay Statements

Detailed pay statements and settlement summaries are available through BackOfficeFleet for all drivers and owner-operators. These documents provide comprehensive breakdowns of earnings, deductions, and net pay.

4. Mandatory Deductions
4.1 Federal and State Taxes

All required federal and state payroll taxes are withheld in accordance with current tax laws and regulations. This includes federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and applicable state taxes. Tax withholding calculations are processed through BackOfficeFleet using current tax tables.

4.2 Family Support Withholding

Family support and child support withholdings are processed per court orders and state agency requirements. Amounts are calculated based on court-ordered percentages and may vary by state. All family support withholdings are documented in settlement summaries and processed with priority over other deductions.

Important: Family support withholdings take priority over other deductions and are protected by federal law. Changes to withholding amounts require updated court orders and must be submitted to HR immediately.
4.3 Garnishments and Levies

Court-ordered wage garnishments and tax levies are processed in accordance with applicable laws. These deductions are processed with priority and may affect net pay calculations. All garnishment orders are maintained confidentially in employee files.

5. Voluntary Deductions
5.1 Insurance Premiums

Employees may elect to participate in company-sponsored insurance programs including health, dental, vision, and life insurance. Premium contributions are deducted on a pre-tax or post-tax basis as applicable and documented in enrollment forms.

5.2 Retirement Contributions

Eligible employees may contribute to 401(k) retirement plans. Contributions are deducted on a pre-tax basis (traditional 401(k)) or post-tax basis (Roth 401(k)) as elected by the employee. Company matching contributions are processed according to plan provisions.

5.3 Other Voluntary Deductions

Additional voluntary deductions may include flexible spending accounts (FSA), health savings accounts (HSA), credit union payments, and other employee-elected deductions. All voluntary deductions require written authorization from the employee.

6. Owner-Operator Settlements
6.1 Settlement Calculations

Owner-operator settlements are calculated based on load revenue minus applicable deductions. Settlement calculations include fuel advances, insurance premiums, lease payments, and other authorized chargebacks. All settlement calculations are processed through BackOfficeFleet with detailed documentation.

6.2 Deduction Limits and Controls

Deductions exceeding 30% of gross revenue require additional review and approval. Large deduction ratios may indicate calculation errors, unauthorized deductions, or special circumstances that need verification before settlement release. All settlements exceeding deduction thresholds are reviewed by the finance department.

Warning: Settlements with deductions exceeding 30% of gross revenue are automatically flagged for review and may require additional documentation before payment release.
6.3 Chargeback Policies

Owner-operators are responsible for various chargebacks including fuel usage beyond allocated amounts, insurance deductibles, equipment damage, and other authorized deductions. All chargebacks are documented and communicated to owner-operators through BackOfficeFleet.

7. Benefits Administration
7.1 Health and Welfare Benefits

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. offers comprehensive benefits including medical, dental, vision, and life insurance. Benefits administration follows ERISA guidelines and includes annual enrollment periods. All benefit elections are maintained in BackOfficeFleet.

7.2 Retirement Benefits

The company 401(k) plan allows employees to save for retirement with pre-tax contributions. Company matching contributions are available according to plan provisions. All retirement plan activity is tracked through BackOfficeFleet.

8. Payroll Processing and Records
8.1 Processing Systems

All payroll processing is conducted through BackOfficeFleet with proper segregation of duties and internal controls. Payroll data is backed up regularly and maintained for compliance purposes.

8.2 Record Keeping

Payroll records are maintained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with federal and state requirements. Records include pay registers, tax filings, deduction authorizations, and employee acknowledgments. All records are available for audit and employee review.

8.3 Settlement Statement Review and Disputes

Drivers and owner-operators may review their settlement statements through BackOfficeFleet. Any disputes or questions regarding settlements must be submitted in writing within 5 business days of payment receipt. The finance department will review and respond to all disputes within 10 business days.

9. Compliance and Reporting
9.1 Regulatory Compliance

This policy complies with all applicable federal and state labor laws including the Fair Labor Standards Act, state wage and hour laws, and tax regulations. Regular audits ensure continued compliance and accuracy of payroll processing.

9.2 Reporting Requirements

Required payroll tax filings including Form 941, state unemployment tax returns, and W-2/W-1099 forms are processed timely through BackOfficeFleet. All reporting deadlines are monitored and met by the payroll department.

10. Policy Contact and Administration
10.1 Policy Administration

This policy is administered by the Human Resources Department in coordination with the Finance Department. Questions regarding compensation, deductions, or payroll processing should be directed to HR.

10.2 Contact Information

Settlement Disputes: Finance Department at finance@deltatrucking.com

PART II — SETTLEMENT-FOCUSED EXTRACT
Settlement Processing Guidelines

This section provides specific guidance for settlement processing and review procedures relevant to driver and owner-operator compensation. These guidelines supplement the full policy and focus on settlement-specific requirements.

Settlement Review Procedures
Automated Review Triggers
  • Net pay below $500 triggers automatic review
  • Deductions exceeding 30% of gross revenue require approval
  • Family support withholdings are automatically prioritized
  • Large deduction ratios trigger financial review
Accessorial Charges and Driver Share

Accessorial charges including detention, layover, and special handling fees are included in settlement calculations. Driver share percentages are applied according to individual agreements and documented in settlement summaries.

Settlement Reimbursement Requirements

Reimbursements for fuel, tolls, and other authorized expenses are processed through BackOfficeFleet with proper documentation. All reimbursement requests require receipts or supporting documentation and are reviewed for compliance with company policies.
