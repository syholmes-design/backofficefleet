Claims, Proof Packet & Insurance Escalation SOP
SOP Overview

This Claims, Proof Packet & Insurance Escalation SOP establishes the standard operating procedure for identifying, documenting, escalating, and resolving incidents that may affect cargo condition, delivery proof, insurance exposure, dispatch eligibility, settlement release, or factoring packet readiness.

The purpose of this SOP is to protect Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.'s interests, preserve claim recovery rights, maintain insurance and regulatory readiness, and ensure that every claim-related event is supported by a complete operational proof packet.

BOF is used as the operating platform to connect dispatch, safety, maintenance, compliance, settlement, finance, and document workflows. BOF should be treated as the system of record for claim status, proof readiness, settlement holds, and manager action tracking.

Incident Intake and Claim Trigger

A claim review must be opened when any event creates possible exposure to cargo loss, cargo damage, delivery dispute, customer chargeback, insurance claim, safety violation, seal mismatch, missing proof, or settlement hold.

Trigger Required Action
Cargo damage reported Open claim review, photograph damage, preserve BOL/POD, and notify manager.
Seal mismatch or broken seal Escalate to dispatch, safety, claims, and insurance review if cargo integrity is affected.
Missing POD, BOL, or delivery signature Place settlement release on hold until required proof is recovered or approved by management.
Accident, collision, theft, cargo shift, or safety event Open safety event, collect evidence, obtain driver statement, and determine insurance exposure.
Customer dispute or short payment Attach invoice, rate confirmation, BOL, POD, accessorial support, and customer correspondence.
Required Claim Packet Contents

A claim packet must be assembled before the claim is closed, submitted to insurance, released for settlement, or included in a factoring packet. The packet should include all documents reasonably available for the event.

  • Incident report Detailed incident documentation with timeline and location
  • Driver statement First-hand account of events and immediate actions taken
  • Witness statement, if available Third-party observations and contact information
  • Photos, dashcam stills, or video evidence Time-stamped visual documentation of damage and conditions
  • Bill of Lading Original signed BOL with condition notes
  • Proof of Delivery Signed POD with delivery confirmation and any exceptions noted
  • Rate confirmation Original rate confirmation with terms and conditions
  • Customer invoice Invoice with charges and any disputed items highlighted
  • Seal verification record Seal numbers and verification at pickup and delivery
  • RFID or dock proof record, if available RFID scan logs and dock proof documentation
  • Repair estimate or damage assessment Professional repair estimates and damage valuations
  • Police report, if applicable Official police report for accidents or theft incidents
  • Insurance notification Formal insurance notice and claim number assignment
  • Claim notes and adjuster communication All correspondence with insurance adjusters and claim notes
  • Settlement hold record Documentation of any settlement holds and release conditions
  • Factoring packet exception note, if applicable Exception language for factoring packet submission
Cargo Damage and Proof Documentation

Cargo damage must be documented with clear, time-stamped photos before cargo is moved, repaired, disposed of, or transferred. Whenever possible, photos should show the cargo, trailer condition, seal, pallet configuration, packaging damage, and identifying labels or shipment numbers.

Evidence Type Purpose
Pre-trip cargo photo Shows condition before departure or at pickup.
Post-trip cargo photo Shows condition at delivery.
Seal pickup photo Confirms seal number and trailer integrity at origin.
Seal delivery photo Confirms seal number and trailer integrity at delivery.
Empty trailer photo Confirms cargo was fully unloaded and no freight remained onboard.
Damage photo packet Supports claim value, customer dispute review, and insurance submission.
RFID / Seal Mismatch Escalation

Any RFID exception, missing scan, seal mismatch, or geofence irregularity must be reviewed before the load is treated as proof-complete. BOF should flag the event and connect it to the related load, driver, asset, settlement, and claim record.

A seal or RFID exception may require dispatch review, insurance review, customer notification, settlement hold, or factoring packet exception language.

Dispatch Hold Rules

Dispatch may be blocked or placed into manager review when an active claim, safety event, cargo proof gap, missing photo, maintenance issue, compliance issue, or RFID/seal exception affects the driver, load, or asset.

A dispatch hold should identify the affected load, driver, trailer or asset, reason for the hold, required fix, responsible owner, and release condition.

Settlement Hold Rules

Settlement release may be held when required proof is incomplete or when an unresolved incident affects payment accuracy, chargeback exposure, claim recovery, customer invoice approval, or factoring packet readiness.

Settlement Hold Trigger Release Condition
Missing POD or delivery signature POD recovered or manager override documented.
Missing BOL BOL uploaded and matched to load record.
Open cargo claim Claim exposure reviewed and hold amount approved.
Seal mismatch Seal evidence reviewed and exception resolved.
Accessorial dispute Lumper, detention, layover, or toll support attached.
Factoring Packet Impact

A post-trip factoring packet should not be marked ready for submission unless the required invoice, rate confirmation, BOL, POD, delivery proof, and accessorial support are complete.

If a claim, cargo exception, missing POD, missing BOL, settlement hold, seal mismatch, or customer dispute exists, BOF should flag the factoring packet as pending review or on hold until the finance manager approves submission.

Insurance Notice and Claim Exposure Bands

Claims should be assigned an exposure band to help determine urgency, reporting requirements, settlement impact, and insurance escalation.

Exposure Band Example Action
Low Minor cargo scuff, no delivery refusal, no injury Document internally and monitor for customer dispute.
Medium Documented cargo damage, accessorial dispute, late claim notice Open claim packet and manager review.
High Rejected load, accident, major cargo damage, theft, injury, or possible litigation Notify insurance, preserve evidence, and escalate to management immediately.
Driver Statement and Corrective Action

When a driver is involved in a safety event, cargo event, seal mismatch, claim, or customer dispute, a driver statement should be requested and linked to the claim record. The statement should describe what happened, timeline, location, parties involved, photos taken, and any immediate corrective action.

If the event is preventable or safety-related, BOF should create a coaching or corrective-action record and link it to the driver safety profile.

BOF Workflow Integration

BOF should connect each claim event across the operating workflow so managers can see the full effect on dispatch, safety, settlement, finance, and compliance.

  1. A safety event, cargo exception, seal mismatch, or customer dispute creates a claim review.
  2. The claim review creates a proof packet requirement.
  3. Missing POD, BOL, seal photo, cargo photo, or driver statement creates a settlement hold.
  4. RFID or seal mismatch creates dispatch review and possible insurance escalation.
  5. Approved evidence releases settlement or factoring packet submission.
  6. Closed claim updates safety, dispatch, settlement, finance, and document records.
Claim Closure and Record Updates

A claim may be closed only after evidence has been reviewed, financial impact has been determined, required insurance or customer notices have been completed, settlement holds have been resolved or documented, and any driver corrective action has been closed or assigned.

The closing record should include claim outcome, amount paid or reserved, deductible impact, recovery/subrogation status, responsible manager, closure date, and any lessons learned.
