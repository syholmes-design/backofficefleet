Regulatory requirement 49 CFR 396.17 addresses periodic inspection. 49 CFR 396.19 and 396.21 address inspector qualifications and recordkeeping. Confirm current eCFR text, inspector qualification, and equivalent-inspection programs (for example certain state inspections) before treating this form as complete legal coverage.

Company policy Each power unit and trailing unit in service must have a current periodic inspection record in the equipment file or an equivalent document the company accepts after Safety/Maintenance review.

Legal / compliance review required This form does not reproduce the Appendix G inspection procedure or declare a 12-month interval as the only lawful option. Equivalent inspections may apply.

Daily DVIR / pre-trip (BOF-SAF-SOP-001) is a different record class from this periodic inspection. Do not use this form as a trip inspection. Do not treat a File Cabinet demo sample as proof that a unit was inspected.
