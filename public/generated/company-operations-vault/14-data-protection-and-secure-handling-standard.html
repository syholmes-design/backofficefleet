1. Purpose and Scope

This Data Protection and Secure Handling Standard establishes comprehensive requirements for protecting BOF's data assets throughout their lifecycle, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information in compliance with regulatory requirements and industry best practices.

Scope: All electronic and physical data assets, including customer information, employee records, financial data, operational data, intellectual property, and third-party information processed by BOF.

2. Data Classification Framework
  • Public Data General business information, no special protection required
  • Internal Data Company internal use, employee access only
  • Confidential Data Sensitive business information, restricted access controls
  • Personal Data Employee and customer personal information, privacy controls
3. Technical Protection Requirements
  • Encryption Standards AES-256 encryption for data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Access Controls Multi-factor authentication, least privilege, and role-based access controls
  • Data Minimization Collect only necessary data and retain for minimum required time
  • Secure Storage Encrypted storage with regular backup, testing, and geo-redundancy
  • Secure Transmission Secure channels, VPN, and encrypted protocols for data transmission
  • Key Management Centralized key management with secure key rotation and storage
4. Data Lifecycle Management

Data protection controls must be applied throughout the entire data lifecycle from creation to disposal.

  • Data Collection Authorized collection with documented purpose, consent, and lawful basis
  • Data Processing Secure processing with audit trails, validation, and integrity checks
  • Data Storage Secure storage with encryption, access controls, and regular backups
  • Data Sharing Authorized sharing only with proper agreements, controls, and data protection
  • Data Archival Secure archival with retention schedules and controlled access
  • Data Disposal Secure disposal with verification, documentation, and certificate of destruction
5. Incident Response and Breach Management

A structured incident response process must be followed for all data breaches and security incidents.

  • Breach Detection 24/7 monitoring systems, automated alerts, and rapid breach identification
  • Incident Assessment Immediate assessment of scope, impact, and affected data subjects
  • Containment Immediate containment to prevent further data loss and system compromise
  • Notification Timely notification to affected parties, regulators, and authorities per legal requirements
  • Recovery Data recovery, system restoration, and post-incident security improvements
  • Post-Mortem Root cause analysis, lessons learned, and process improvements
6. Regulatory Compliance and Legal Requirements

BOF must comply with applicable data protection regulations and legal requirements across all jurisdictions.

  • Regulatory Compliance GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, DOT regulations, and state privacy laws
  • Audit Requirements Quarterly internal audits, annual external assessments, and compliance reporting
  • Documentation Comprehensive documentation of data handling procedures and compliance evidence
  • Training Requirements Annual data protection training and role-specific security awareness
  • Privacy by Design Privacy considerations built into systems and processes from inception
7. BOF System Integration

Data protection controls must be integrated across all BOF systems and business processes.

  • Dispatch Systems Secure handling of driver data, load information, and location data with privacy controls
  • Payroll Systems Protection of employee personal data, financial information, and tax records
  • Document Management Secure storage, version control, and access controls for all company documents
  • Driver Portal Secure driver authentication, data privacy, and mobile application security
  • Customer Systems Protection of customer data, shipment information, and business communications
8. Data Subject Rights

BOF must respect and facilitate data subject rights in accordance with applicable privacy regulations.

Data Subject Rights:

  • Right to access personal data held by BOF
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten) where applicable
  • Right to restriction of processing under certain conditions
  • Right to data portability for personal data
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to lodge complaints with supervisory authorities
9. Third-Party and Vendor Management

Third parties and vendors processing BOF data must meet equivalent data protection standards.

  • Due Diligence Comprehensive security and privacy assessments before engagement
  • Data Processing Agreements Legally binding agreements with data protection obligations
  • Ongoing Monitoring Regular monitoring and audits of vendor compliance
  • Data Transfer Mechanisms Appropriate safeguards for international data transfers
10. Monitoring and Continuous Improvement

Regular monitoring and continuous improvement processes ensure ongoing data protection effectiveness.

  • Security Monitoring 24/7 security monitoring with automated threat detection and response
  • Compliance Monitoring Continuous compliance monitoring with automated controls and reporting
  • Risk Assessments Regular risk assessments with mitigation plans and progress tracking
  • Policy Reviews Semi-annual policy reviews with updates based on threat landscape changes
