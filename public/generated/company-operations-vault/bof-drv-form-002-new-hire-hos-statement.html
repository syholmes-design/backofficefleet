Regulatory requirement 49 CFR 395.8 addresses driver’s record of duty status. When a motor carrier uses a driver for the first time, the carrier must obtain a signed statement of the total time on duty during the immediately preceding 7 days and the time at which the driver was last relieved from duty. Confirm current eCFR text, including any ELD or short-haul exceptions, before treating this form as complete legal coverage.

Company policy Do not first-dispatch a newly onboarded driver until this statement is on file or the BOF onboarding record already captures the same 7-day on-duty information.

Legal / compliance review required This form does not calculate remaining drive hours and is not an ELD. Prefer the ELD / HOS system of record after the driver is enrolled.
