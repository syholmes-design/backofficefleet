1. Purpose

Every controlled document answers: why it exists, who owns it, who maintains it, who reviews it, who approves it, which workflow it supports, what evidence it creates, and when it is superseded. A file is not approved merely because it was uploaded.

2. Document code

Format: BOF-[FUNCTION]-[TYPE]-[SEQUENCE]. Sequence is stable. Revision is a separate Version field.

If an application path already exists (for example 10-safety-compliance-governance-policy.html or DAT_SOP_CLM_001_Rev3_1.pdf), keep that path. Overlay the controlled code in metadata.

3. Lifecycle

DRAFT → INTERNAL REVIEW → COMPLIANCE REVIEW (when required) → APPROVAL → ACTIVE → REVISION → SUPERSEDED → ARCHIVED.

A superseded version must not be presented as ACTIVE.

4. Roles

  • Owner — substantive accuracy and business purpose.
  • Steward — metadata, revision history, distribution.
  • Compliance reviewer — regulatory review when the document states legal requirements.
  • Approver — authorized to place the document in ACTIVE status.

The same person may hold more than one role. Content ownership is still distinct from file administration.

5. Digital behavior

Prefer structured BOF records over duplicate Word/PDF when the system already captures the fact (settlement totals, dispatch holds, pickup authorization). Static templates remain for policies, acknowledgments, and human-completed checklists.

6. Vault classes

DRIVER-OWNED · EMPLOYER RECORD · OPERATIONAL RECORD · COMPLIANCE RECORD · FINANCIAL RECORD · SYSTEM-GENERATED RECORD.

7. Authority labels

Documents that mention law must distinguish Regulatory requirement, BOF/company policy, and operational best practice. Unverified statements are flagged for legal/compliance review.

8. Current vault documents (presentation layer)

Historical and impostor copies live under _historical/ and are not current. _generator-preview/ is not the vault surface.
