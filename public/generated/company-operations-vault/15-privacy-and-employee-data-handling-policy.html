Section 2 — Recovered BOF operating constraints

Source: recovered public privacy notice for BOF record handling. This is company operating practice recovered from BOF text, not a cited statute.

  • Do not send driver medical, license, tax, banking, insurance, customer, credential, or claim records through an unsolicited public email.
  • This product should not be used as a secure document-upload channel for unsolicited public contact.
  • Static demo records are intended to be synthetic and are not presented as live customer records.
  • Privacy or accessibility questions may be asked without sending sensitive fleet or personal records.
  • This notice documents recovered BOF handling language and is not a substitute for review by qualified privacy counsel.

Section 3 — Recovered catalog context

The DAT-IT catalog treats driver data, document handling, BOL images, load documents, and operational records as classified operating information under DAT-IT-003 and DAT-IT-004. Employee/driver file contents remain evidence records (CDL, MEC, MVR, I-9, W-9, bank) rather than a new BOF privacy form pack.
