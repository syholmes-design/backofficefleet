Purpose

This policy establishes procedures for maintaining audit-ready records, organizing operational documentation, and ensuring compliance with tax and regulatory requirements. BOF organizes the operational records that support audit readiness — load records, trip miles, fuel costs, tolls, receipts, asset schedules, invoices, settlements, proof bundles, and document retention controls.

Scope

This policy applies to all financial and operational records that support tax reporting, regulatory compliance, and audit requirements. This includes but is not limited to:

  • Load records and trip documentation
  • Fuel purchases and mileage tracking
  • Toll receipts and lumper documentation
  • Asset schedules and depreciation records
  • Invoices and accounts receivable
  • Driver settlements and payroll records
  • Proof of delivery bundles
  • Insurance and claims documentation

Responsibilities

Finance Team

  • Maintain audit-ready financial records
  • Ensure proper documentation retention
  • Coordinate tax reporting requirements
  • Support audit inquiries and documentation requests

Operations Team

  • Complete load documentation requirements
  • Ensure proof of delivery bundles are complete
  • Maintain accurate mileage and fuel records
  • Provide operational data for audit support

Compliance Team

  • Monitor regulatory requirements
  • Ensure documentation meets audit standards
  • Coordinate audit preparation activities
  • Maintain regulatory compliance records

Audit Readiness Workflow

1. Document Collection

  • Collect all required documentation for each load
  • Verify completeness of proof bundles
  • Ensure proper categorization and filing
  • Maintain electronic and physical copies as required

2. Record Organization

  • Organize records by load, date, and category
  • Maintain cross-references between related documents
  • Ensure proper indexing and searchability
  • Implement document retention schedules

3. Quality Assurance

  • Review documentation for completeness and accuracy
  • Verify proper authorization and signatures
  • Ensure compliance with retention requirements
  • Address any documentation gaps or issues

4. Audit Support

  • Respond to audit requests promptly
  • Provide organized documentation packages
  • Support audit interviews and inquiries
  • Address audit findings and recommendations

Document Retention Requirements

Financial Records

  • Tax returns: 7 years
  • Financial statements: 7 years
  • Invoices and receipts: 7 years
  • Bank statements: 7 years
  • Payroll records: 7 years

Operational Records

  • Load records: 7 years
  • Proof of delivery: 7 years
  • Fuel receipts: 7 years
  • Toll receipts: 7 years
  • Maintenance records: 7 years

Compliance Records

  • Safety inspections: 7 years
  • Driver qualifications: 7 years
  • Insurance policies: 7 years
  • Regulatory filings: 7 years
  • Audit reports: 7 years

Audit Support Checklist

Load Record Requirements

  • Complete load information (origin, destination, dates)
  • Customer and broker documentation
  • Rate confirmation and agreements
  • Delivery confirmation and signatures
  • Exception documentation (if applicable)

Mileage and Fuel Support

  • Trip mileage logs and documentation
  • Fuel purchase receipts with dates and amounts
  • Fuel card statements and reconciliation
  • IFTA reporting documentation
  • State fuel tax filings

Toll and Lumper Support

  • Toll receipts with dates and amounts
  • Lumper receipts and authorization
  • Reimbursement documentation
  • Approval records for expenses
  • Vendor invoices and payments

Proof Bundle Requirements

  • Proof of delivery documents
  • Time-stamped delivery photos
  • Customer acceptance confirmation
  • Exception documentation (if applicable)
  • Digital and physical copies maintained

Invoice and Receivable Support

  • Complete invoice documentation
  • Customer purchase orders
  • Rate confirmation agreements
  • Factoring documentation (if applicable)
  • Payment records and reconciliation

Settlement and Payroll Support

  • Driver settlement calculations
  • Payroll records and tax filings
  • Deduction and reimbursement documentation
  • Owner-operator agreements
  • Worker classification documentation

Asset and Debt Support

  • Asset purchase agreements
  • Financing and lease documentation
  • Depreciation schedules
  • Insurance policies and claims
  • Maintenance and repair records

Review Cadence

Monthly Reviews

  • Verify retention compliance

  • Update audit readiness procedures

Quarterly Reviews

  • Conduct audit readiness assessments
  • Review regulatory compliance updates
  • Update documentation requirements

Annual Reviews

  • Policy and procedure updates
  • Training and education requirements
  • Technology and system improvements

BOF is positioned as audit-ready support and management financial intelligence, not as final tax/accounting advice.
