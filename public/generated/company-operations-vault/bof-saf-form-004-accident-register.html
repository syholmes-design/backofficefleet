Regulatory requirement 49 CFR 390.15 addresses assistance in investigations and special studies, including an accident register. Confirm current eCFR text for the definition of “accident” in 49 CFR 390.5 / 390.5T and for required register data elements before treating this layout as complete.

Company policy Safety also logs near-misses and non-DOT incidents in BOF-SAF-FORM-002. Those company events are not automatically “accidents” for 390.15.

Legal / compliance review required Do not copy a third-party accident-register product. Do not fabricate fatalities, injuries, or hazmat releases. Demo claim files (for example load L003) are samples, not this register.

Prefer a structured BOF safety event record if one exists. This register is the paper/PDF counterpart and a completeness view. It does not authorize claims settlement or dispatch decisions.
