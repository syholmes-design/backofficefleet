Safety Responsibilities

Driver Safety Responsibilities

I understand and agree to the following safety responsibilities:

  • Comply with all federal, state, and local regulations
  • Follow all company safety policies and procedures
  • Conduct thorough vehicle inspections before each trip
  • Report any vehicle defects or safety concerns immediately
  • Maintain accurate logbooks and documentation
  • Never operate a vehicle while impaired by drugs, alcohol, or medication
  • Adhere to hours of service regulations at all times
  • Wear seat belts at all times while operating vehicles
  • Secure all cargo properly according to regulations
  • Report all accidents and incidents immediately
  • Participate in ongoing safety training and education
  • Maintain a professional and safe driving record

Personal Safety Commitment
