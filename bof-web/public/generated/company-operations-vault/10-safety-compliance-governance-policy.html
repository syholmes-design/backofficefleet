Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
Safety and Compliance Governance Policy
Policy Overview

This Safety and Compliance Governance Policy establishes the framework for maintaining a safe, compliant, and efficient operation while protecting BOF's assets, employees, and reputation. It ensures adherence to regulatory requirements and promotes a culture of safety throughout the organization.

Safety Management System
  • Pre-Trip Inspections Comprehensive vehicle inspections before departure with documented safety checks
  • Post-Trip Reviews Driver safety performance evaluation and route compliance assessment
  • Incident Reporting Immediate reporting of accidents, injuries, and safety violations with detailed documentation
  • Safety Training Requirements Mandatory safety training and certification for all drivers
  • Drug and Alcohol Policy Zero tolerance policy with DOT-compliant random testing procedures
  • Hours of Service Compliance Strict adherence to federal and state hours-of-service regulations
  • Vehicle Maintenance Standards Regular preventive maintenance schedules and immediate repair for safety issues
Compliance Framework
  • Regulatory Compliance DOT, FMCSA, OSHA, and state transportation regulations adherence
  • Risk Assessment Regular risk identification, mitigation planning, and safety protocol reviews
  • Audit Procedures Quarterly safety audits, compliance reviews, and corrective action tracking
  • Documentation Requirements Comprehensive safety documentation and incident reporting procedures
Driver Safety Standards
  • Personal Safety Personal responsibility for safety equipment use and adherence to company protocols
  • Vehicle Safety Proper vehicle operation, daily inspections, and immediate reporting of safety issues
  • Load Safety Secure loading procedures, weight distribution compliance, and cargo securement standards
  • Emergency Procedures Clear emergency response protocols and communication procedures
  • Communication Standards Regular safety meetings, clear reporting channels, and proactive hazard communication
Compliance Monitoring
  • Performance Metrics Safety incident rates, compliance scores, and improvement tracking
  • Safety Culture Continuous safety training, open communication, and proactive hazard identification
  • Technology Integration Electronic logging systems, telematics integration, and automated safety monitoring
Policy Administration
  • Version Control Annual policy reviews, updates based on regulatory changes, and continuous improvement
  • Training Requirements Annual safety certification and compliance training for all personnel
  • System Integration Integration with dispatch, payroll, and compliance monitoring systems
