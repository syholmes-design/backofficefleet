Policy Overview

This AI Use and Automation Governance Policy establishes guidelines for responsible AI tool use, automation implementation, and data processing while maintaining security, privacy, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

AI Use Principles
  • Business Purpose AI tools used for legitimate business purposes only
  • Human Oversight Human review required for all AI-generated decisions
  • Data Privacy No public/personal AI tools for Restricted/Confidential data
  • Transparency Clear disclosure of AI use and data processing
  • Accountability Clear responsibility for AI systems and decisions
3. Approved AI Applications and Use Cases

BOF maintains an approved inventory of AI tools and applications that have undergone security and compliance review.

  • Document Processing Company-approved document analysis, summarization, and classification tools
  • Data Analysis Approved business intelligence, analytics, and reporting platforms
  • Route Optimization Company-approved routing, logistics optimization, and fuel efficiency tools
  • Predictive Maintenance Approved predictive maintenance, vehicle health monitoring, and scheduling systems
  • Customer Service Approved customer communication, chatbots, and support automation
  • Fraud Detection AI-powered fraud detection and anomaly identification systems
4. Prohibited AI Use and Restrictions

Certain AI applications and use cases are prohibited due to security, privacy, or regulatory concerns.

  • Personal AI Tools No unauthorized personal AI tools on company systems or devices
  • Public AI Services No public AI services for Restricted/Confidential data processing
  • Unapproved Automation No automation without proper security review and approval
  • Data Training No training AI models on company data without explicit authorization
  • Vendor AI Tools No vendor AI tools without security assessment and approval
  • Biometric AI No biometric data processing without explicit consent and compliance
5. Human Oversight and Decision Making

Human oversight is required for all AI-generated decisions and recommendations to ensure accountability and accuracy.

  • Human Review Required All AI summaries, classifications, and recommendations require human review
  • No Automated Decisions No final HR, payroll, safety, disciplinary, compliance, driver eligibility, dispatch-blocking, or financial decisions by AI alone
  • Document Extraction Human verification of all AI-Operational Record extractions and classifications
  • Calculation Verification Human verification of all AI-generated calculations and financial recommendations
  • Critical Decision Review Multi-level human review for high-impact AI recommendations
6. Security and Privacy Controls

Comprehensive security controls protect AI systems, data, and outputs from unauthorized access and misuse.

  • Access Controls Multi-factor authentication and least privilege for AI system access
  • Data Encryption End-to-end encryption for all AI-processed data and outputs
  • Audit Logging Comprehensive logging of AI system access, usage, and decisions
  • Prompt Management Secure prompt storage, version control, and access logging
  • Output Handling Secure handling, classification, and review of AI-generated outputs
  • Model Security Secure model storage, versioning, and access controls
7. Cloud AI Services and SaaS Controls

Cloud AI services must meet strict security and compliance requirements before being approved for use.

  • Access Management Centralized access control and identity management for cloud AI services
  • Data Residency Controlled data residency and jurisdiction compliance for cloud AI
  • Vendor Assessment Comprehensive security assessment for all AI service vendors
  • Exit Planning Data extraction and migration planning for service termination
  • Service Level Agreements SLAs with security, privacy, and availability requirements
8. Data Protection and Training Restrictions

Strict controls govern how company data can be used for AI training and model development.

  • Data Training Prohibition No training AI models on company data without explicit authorization
  • Vendor AI Restrictions Prohibit vendors from using company data for AI training without consent
  • Data Sharing Limits Strict controls on data sharing with AI service providers
  • Anonymization Requirements Data anonymization and pseudonymization for AI training datasets
  • Consent Management Explicit consent requirements for data used in AI training
9. BOF System Integration and Use Cases

AI applications are integrated with BOF systems to enhance operational efficiency and decision support.

  • Dispatch Integration AI tools for route optimization, fuel efficiency, and dispatch decision support
  • Driver Data Protection AI-assisted driver data analysis with privacy controls and human oversight
  • Document Management AI-powered document classification, extraction, and workflow automation
  • Financial Analysis AI tools for financial data analysis with human verification
  • Safety Monitoring AI-assisted safety monitoring and risk identification systems
10. Incident Management and Response

Specific incident response procedures address AI-related security and privacy incidents.

  • AI Incidents Rapid reporting of AI-related security and privacy incidents
  • Data Breaches Immediate reporting of AI-related data breaches and exposures
  • Privacy Violations Reporting mechanisms for AI-related privacy violations
  • Model Failures Procedures for AI model failures, bias detection, and corrections
  • Security Issues Escalation procedures for AI-related security issues and vulnerabilities
11. Training and Awareness

Regular training ensures employees understand AI governance requirements and responsible use practices.

Training Requirements:

  • Annual AI governance and responsible use training for all employees
  • Role-specific training for AI system developers and operators
  • Security awareness training focused on AI-related risks and threats
  • Regular updates on new AI tools, policies, and procedures
  • Incident response training for AI-related security events
