Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
AI Use and Automation Governance Policy
Policy Overview

This AI Use and Automation Governance Policy establishes guidelines for responsible AI tool use, automation implementation, and data processing while maintaining security, privacy, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

AI Use Principles
  • Business Purpose AI tools used for legitimate business purposes only
  • Human Oversight Human review required for all AI-generated decisions
  • Data Privacy No public/personal AI tools for Restricted/Confidential data
  • Transparency Clear disclosure of AI use and data processing
  • Accountability Clear responsibility for AI systems and decisions
Approved AI Tools
  • Document Processing Company-approved document analysis and summarization tools
  • Data Analysis Approved business intelligence and analytics platforms
  • Route Optimization Company-approved routing and logistics optimization tools
  • Predictive Maintenance Approved predictive maintenance and scheduling systems
  • Customer Service Approved customer communication and support automation
Prohibited AI Use
  • Personal AI Tools No unauthorized personal AI tools on company systems
  • Public AI Services No public AI services for Restricted/Confidential data
  • Unapproved Automation No automation without proper security review and approval
  • Data Training No training AI models on company data without authorization
  • Vendor AI Tools No vendor AI tools without security assessment and approval
Data Processing Requirements
  • Human Review Required All AI summaries, classifications, and recommendations require human review
  • No Automated Decisions No final HR, payroll, safety, disciplinary, compliance, driver eligibility, dispatch-blocking, or financial decisions by AI alone
  • Document Extraction Human verification of all AI-generated document extractions
  • Calculation Verification Human verification of all AI-generated calculations and recommendations
Security and Privacy Controls
  • Access Controls MFA and least privilege for AI system access
  • Data Encryption Encryption for all AI-processed data and outputs
  • Audit Logging Comprehensive logging of AI system access and usage
  • Prompt Management Secure prompt storage and version control
  • Output Handling Secure handling and review of AI-generated outputs
SaaS and Cloud Controls
  • Access Management Centralized access control for cloud AI services
  • Data Residency Controlled data residency and jurisdiction compliance
  • Vendor Assessment Security assessment for all AI service vendors
  • Exit Planning Data extraction and migration planning for service termination
Mobile Device and Browser Controls
  • Device Management MDM for all devices accessing AI systems
  • Browser Restrictions No unauthorized browser extensions or AI tools
  • Remote Access Secure remote access with additional controls for AI systems
Vendor and Data Training Restrictions
  • Data Training Prohibition No training AI models on company data without explicit authorization
  • Vendor AI Restrictions Prohibit vendors from using company data for AI training without consent
  • Data Sharing Limits Strict controls on data sharing with AI service providers
Retention, Deletion, and Export
  • Data Retention Defined retention periods for AI-processed data and outputs
  • Secure Deletion Secure deletion procedures for AI data and models
  • Export Controls Controlled export of AI-generated data and documents
  • Subprocessor Review Regular review of AI subprocessors and data handlers
Incident Reporting
  • AI Incidents Rapid reporting of AI-related security and privacy incidents
  • Data Breaches Immediate reporting of AI-related data breaches
  • Privacy Violations Reporting mechanisms for AI-related privacy violations
  • Security Issues Escalation procedures for AI-related security issues
BOF Integration
  • Dispatch Integration AI tools for route optimization and dispatch decision support
  • Driver Data Protection AI-assisted driver data analysis with privacy controls
  • Document Management AI-powered document classification and management
  • Financial Analysis AI tools for financial data analysis with human oversight
