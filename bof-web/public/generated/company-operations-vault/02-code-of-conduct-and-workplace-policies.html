Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
Code of Conduct and Workplace Policies
Policy Overview

This Code of Conduct and Workplace Policies document establishes behavioral standards, professional conduct expectations, and workplace procedures that maintain a safe, productive, and respectful environment for all BOF employees and drivers.

Professional Conduct
  • Integrity and Honesty Maintain truthful communication, accurate record-keeping, and ethical decision-making in all business dealings.
  • Professionalism and Reliability Report to work on time, follow through on commitments, and represent BOF professionally in all interactions.
  • Safety and Compliance Follow all safety protocols, comply with regulations, and maintain proper documentation for all operations.
  • Respect and Teamwork Treat colleagues and customers with respect, collaborate effectively, and contribute to positive work environment.
Workplace Policies
  • Anti-Harassment and Discrimination Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation. Report concerns through proper channels.
  • Drug and Alcohol Policy Drug-free workplace with zero tolerance for substance use. Random testing may be conducted per DOT regulations.
  • Workplace Safety Follow established safety procedures, report incidents immediately, and maintain hazard-free environment.
  • Communication Standards Use professional language, respond promptly to communications, and maintain accurate documentation.
  • Technology and Equipment Use Use company equipment responsibly, follow technology policies, and report maintenance needs promptly.
Disciplinary Procedures
  • Progressive Discipline Verbal warnings, written warnings, and performance improvement plans before termination consideration.
  • Termination Policy At-will employment with proper notice periods and final paycheck processing per state law.
Employee Rights
  • Equal Employment Opportunity Fair hiring practices, equal pay for equal work, and non-discriminatory employment decisions.
  • Wage and Hour Laws Compliance with federal and state wage, hour, and overtime regulations.
  • Family and Medical Leave FMLA compliance, reasonable accommodation, and protected leave policies.
  • Workplace Accommodations Reasonable accommodations for qualified employees with disabilities or medical conditions.
Reporting Procedures
  • Incident Reporting Report workplace incidents, safety concerns, or policy violations through immediate supervisor or HR channels.
  • Confidentiality Maintain confidentiality of company information and trade secrets as outlined in employment agreements.
  • Open Door Policy Open communication with management about workplace concerns, safety issues, or operational improvements.
