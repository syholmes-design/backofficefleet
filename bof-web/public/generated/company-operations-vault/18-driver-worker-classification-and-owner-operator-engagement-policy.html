Driver Worker Classification and Owner-Operator Engagement Policy
Policy Overview

This Driver Worker Classification and Owner-Operator Engagement Policy establishes standardized procedures for properly classifying drivers as employees, independent contractors, owner-operators, or third-party/leased drivers. This policy ensures compliance with labor regulations, proper tax treatment, and appropriate operational controls while maintaining flexibility for different driver engagement models.

Worker Classification Framework
  • Classification Criteria Behavioral control, economic dependence, equipment provision, and operational integration
  • Employee Driver Works exclusively for BOF, follows BOF schedules, uses BOF equipment, subject to BOF policies and procedures
  • Independent Contractor Provides services to BOF under contract, maintains own insurance, handles own taxes, not eligible for BOF benefits
  • Owner-Operator Owns/leases equipment, contracts with BOF, may hire additional drivers, responsible for equipment maintenance and insurance
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Works through carrier or leasing company, not directly employed by BOF, carrier maintains primary insurance
Classification Review Process
  • Initial Classification Based on driver application, work history, and equipment ownership
  • Periodic Review Quarterly review of driver classifications based on operational changes
  • Misclassification Resolution Formal process for addressing and correcting classification errors
Onboarding Requirements
  • Employee Driver CDL, medical card, MVR, I-9, W-4, employee handbook acknowledgment, payroll setup, direct deposit
  • Independent Contractor Independent contractor agreement, insurance certificate, bank setup
  • Owner-Operator Owner-operator lease agreement, insurance certificate, equipment schedule
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier agreement, insurance certificate, driver qualification confirmation
Payroll and Tax Treatment
  • Employee Driver W-2 tax withholding, BOF benefits, workers compensation coverage
  • Independent Contractor 1099 reporting, responsible for own taxes
  • Owner-Operator 1099 reporting, responsible for own taxes and per-mile payments
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier handles tax reporting, per-mile payments to driver
Benefits and Coverage
  • Employee Driver Eligible for health insurance, retirement plan, workers compensation
  • Independent Contractor Not eligible for BOF benefits, must maintain own coverage
  • Owner-Operator May be eligible for BOF benefits depending on lease terms
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Not eligible for BOF benefits, coverage through carrier or leasing company
Equipment and Maintenance
  • Employee Driver BOF provides equipment, responsible for maintenance
  • Independent Contractor Contractor responsible for equipment maintenance and insurance
  • Owner-Operator Owner-operator responsible for equipment ownership, maintenance, and insurance
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier or leasing company responsible for equipment
Insurance Requirements
  • Employee Driver BOF provides occupational accident coverage
  • Independent Contractor Contractor must maintain own occupational accident coverage
  • Owner-Operator Owner-operator must maintain coverage for all drivers
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier or leasing company provides primary coverage
Safety and Compliance
  • Employee Driver BOF safety policies apply, FMCSA compliance required
  • Independent Contractor Contractor must comply with BOF safety requirements
  • Owner-Operator Owner-operator must comply with BOF safety standards and maintain driver qualifications
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Must follow carrier safety policies and maintain FMCSA compliance
Dispatch and Operational Control
  • Employee Driver Dispatched through BOF system, follows BOF routes and schedules
  • Independent Contractor May receive dispatch through BOF if contract allows
  • Owner-Operator Dispatches own drivers, coordinates with BOF for load assignments
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Dispatched through carrier system, coordinates with BOF as needed
Chargebacks and Financial Transactions
  • Employee Driver Direct deposit, eligible for fuel cards and advances
  • Independent Contractor Handles own transactions, may be eligible for selected advances
  • Owner-Operator Responsible for fuel cards, tolls, and per-mile payments
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier handles financial transactions, coordinates settlements with BOF
Document and Record Requirements
  • Employee Driver Maintains driver qualification file, provides emergency contacts
  • Independent Contractor Provides required documentation, maintains qualification status
  • Owner-Operator Maintains driver files, provides qualification updates to BOF
  • Third-Party/Leased Driver Carrier maintains driver records, provides qualification status to BOF
BOF Data Model Implications
  • Worker Type Field Must support employee driver, independent contractor, owner-operator, and third-party/leased driver classifications
  • Payroll Integration Different tax treatments and benefit eligibility based on worker classification
  • Compliance Tracking Safety and compliance requirements apply to all driver types
  • Settlement Processing Settlement calculations and payment routing vary by worker classification
