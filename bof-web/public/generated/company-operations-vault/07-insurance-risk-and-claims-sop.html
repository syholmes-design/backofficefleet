Insurance, Risk, and Claims SOP
SOP Overview

This Insurance, Risk, and Claims SOP establishes standardized procedures for managing insurance claims, risk assessment, and safety incident documentation. It ensures consistent handling of all incidents while protecting BOF's interests and maintaining compliance with regulatory requirements.

Claims Management Process
  • Incident Reporting Report all incidents immediately with detailed documentation and photos
  • Initial Assessment Secure scene, assess damage, and document initial findings within 24 hours
  • Claims Investigation Gather evidence, interview witnesses, and determine liability within 72 hours
  • Damage Documentation Photograph damage, collect repair estimates, and maintain detailed records
  • Settlement Authority Evaluate settlement offers and coordinate with insurance adjusters
  • Subrogation Process Pursue recovery from responsible parties when applicable
Risk Assessment and Prevention
  • Pre-Trip Inspection Document vehicle condition and identify potential hazards before departure
  • Safety Protocols Follow established safety procedures and maintain proper documentation
  • Driver Training Provide ongoing safety training and certification requirements
  • Equipment Maintenance Regular vehicle inspections and preventive maintenance schedules
Insurance Requirements
  • Coverage Verification Confirm adequate insurance coverage for all loads and equipment
  • Certificate of Insurance Maintain current insurance certificates on file for all vehicles
  • Additional Insured Parties List additional insured parties as required by lease agreements
Claims Processing
  • Claim Intake Document claim information, assign claim number, and notify all stakeholders
  • Evidence Collection Gather photos, videos, witness statements, and supporting documentation
  • Claims Administration Track claim status, maintain claim files, and provide regular updates
  • Settlement Coordination Coordinate with insurance adjusters, legal counsel, and factoring companies
Safety Integration
  • Dispatch Communication Real-time claim status updates and safety alerts in dispatch system
  • Safety Alerts Immediate notification of safety incidents and regulatory violations
  • Compliance Reporting Regular safety audits and incident trend analysis
Documentation Requirements
  • Claim Files Maintain organized claim files with proper retention schedules
  • Photographic Evidence Date-stamped photos with detailed damage descriptions
  • Witness Statements Collected witness statements and contact information
  • Repair Estimates Multiple repair quotes with detailed breakdowns
  • Police Reports Official police reports for accidents or theft incidents
Integration with BOF Systems
  • Dispatch Integration Real-time claim status and load information in dispatch system
  • Load Evidence System Digital photos and documents linked to specific loads
  • Settlement System Automated settlement calculations and payment processing
  • Financial Reporting Monthly claims analysis and cost reporting
