Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
Factoring and Receivables Policy
Policy Overview

This Factoring and Receivables Policy establishes guidelines for managing customer invoices, credit terms, and collection procedures. It ensures consistent cash flow management while protecting BOF's financial interests and maintaining positive customer relationships.

Factoring Program Overview

BOF maintains relationships with approved factoring companies to provide immediate cash flow on invoices. Factoring advances are available for qualified loads and are repaid through automatic deductions from customer payments.

Eligibility and Requirements
  • Customer Credit Requirements Minimum credit score of 650, 2+ years payment history, and no outstanding tax liens
  • Load Eligibility Loads over $5,000 with verified customers and proper documentation
  • Documentation Standards Complete bill of lading, signed rate confirmation, and proof of delivery for all factored loads
Factoring Terms and Rates
  • Advance Rates Up to 90% of verified load value at 2% fee structure
  • Factoring Fees Transaction fee: 1-3%, Monthly service fee: $150, Minimum monthly volume: $50,000
  • Recourse Options Recourse factoring available for qualified customers, Non-recourse options for high-volume accounts
  • Payment Terms Net payment within 24 hours of customer receipt, Reserve account maintained for disputed charges
Collection Procedures
  • Invoice Processing Submit invoices to factoring company within 24 hours of load completion
  • Customer Communication Direct customer contact for payment issues, maintain professional relationships
  • Dispute Resolution Document all disputes and involve factoring company in resolution process
  • Credit Management Monitor customer credit status and adjust credit limits based on payment history
Receivables Management
  • Aging Reports Generate monthly aging reports and follow collection procedures
  • Bad Debt Reserve Maintain 2% reserve for potential bad debt losses
  • Concentration Limits No single customer represents more than 15% of total receivables
Integration with BOF Systems
  • Dispatch Integration Real-time invoice status and factoring availability in dispatch system
  • Billing System Automated payment processing and account reconciliation
  • Financial Reporting Monthly factoring reports and receivables aging analysis
Compliance and Risk Management
  • Regulatory Compliance Adherence to UCC Article 9 and state factoring regulations
  • Risk Assessment Regular customer credit evaluation and concentration monitoring
  • Audit Requirements Annual external audit and quarterly internal reviews
Policy Administration
  • Version Control Annual policy review and updates as business needs evolve
  • Training Requirements Annual factoring and collections training for finance and dispatch teams
