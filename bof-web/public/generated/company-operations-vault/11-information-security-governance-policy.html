1. Purpose and Scope

This Information Security Governance Policy establishes the comprehensive framework for protecting BOF's information assets, ensuring regulatory compliance, and maintaining business continuity. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and third parties with access to BOF information systems, data, or facilities.

Scope: All electronic and physical information assets, including but not limited to dispatch systems, payroll data, driver information, financial records, customer data, and supporting infrastructure.

2. Executive Oversight and Accountability

BOF's executive leadership maintains ultimate accountability for information security governance. The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) reports directly to the Chief Technology Officer and provides quarterly security briefings to the Executive Leadership Team and Board of Directors.

Executive Responsibilities:

  • Approve security policies and allocate resources for implementation
  • Ensure compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Review security incident reports and major risk assessments
  • Support security awareness and training initiatives
3. Security Roles and Responsibilities

Clear roles and responsibilities are defined to ensure effective security governance throughout the organization.

4. Security Governance Framework
  • Information Security Management Comprehensive security program with risk assessment and incident response
  • Access Control Role-based access with least privilege principle and regular access reviews
  • Data Classification Confidential, Restricted, Internal, and Public data classification system
  • Security Awareness Training Annual security training for all personnel with phishing awareness
  • Incident Management Structured incident response with 24/7 monitoring and reporting
  • Business Continuity Disaster recovery planning with regular testing and updates
  • Compliance Management Regular compliance audits and adherence to industry standards
5. Incident Reporting and Management

All security incidents must be reported immediately to the Security Operations Center (SOC) via the incident reporting hotline or secure email. Incidents include but are not limited to unauthorized access, data breaches, malware infections, and policy violations.

Incident Response Timeline:

  • Immediate (0-1 hour): Initial assessment and containment
  • Triage (1-4 hours): Investigation and impact analysis
  • Response (4-24 hours): Remediation and recovery
  • Recovery (24-72 hours): System restoration and monitoring
  • Post-incident (1-2 weeks): Root cause analysis and improvement
6. Security Awareness and Training

All personnel must complete mandatory security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter. Additional role-specific training is required for IT staff, managers, and personnel with access to sensitive data.

Training Requirements:

  • Annual security awareness training (all staff)
  • Phishing simulation exercises (quarterly)
  • Data handling and classification training (data handlers)
  • Secure coding practices (development team)
  • Incident response procedures (IT and security staff)
7. Periodic Review and Assessment

This policy and its effectiveness will be reviewed quarterly by the Information Security & Compliance team. Annual comprehensive assessments will be conducted to evaluate the overall security posture and identify areas for improvement.

Review Activities:

  • Policy compliance audits (quarterly)
  • Risk assessments (semi-annual)
  • Penetration testing (annual)
  • Vulnerability assessments (monthly)
  • Security metrics reporting (monthly)
8. Audit and Compliance Monitoring

Regular audits will be conducted to ensure compliance with this policy and applicable regulations. All security controls will be monitored for effectiveness and compliance with established standards.

Compliance Framework:

  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework alignment
  • ISO 27001:2022 controls implementation
  • GDPR and CCPA data protection compliance
  • DOT and FMCSA regulatory requirements
  • Industry-specific transportation security standards
9. Technical Controls
  • Network Security Next-generation firewalls, intrusion prevention, network segmentation, and secure remote access
  • Endpoint Protection Advanced endpoint detection and response, disk encryption, and mobile device management
  • Application Security Secure SDLC, code scanning, web application firewalls, and regular penetration testing
  • Data Encryption AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit, with centralized key management
  • Identity and Access Management Multi-factor authentication, single sign-on, and privileged access management
10. Administrative Controls
  • Security Policies Comprehensive policy suite with regular reviews and updates
  • Personnel Security Background checks, security agreements, and role-based access certification
  • Physical Security Data center access controls, visitor management, and secure document disposal
  • Vendor Security Third-party risk assessments, security clauses in contracts, and regular audits
11. Integration with BOF Systems
  • Dispatch Integration Secure access controls, audit logging, and real-time monitoring for dispatch systems
  • Payroll Integration Encrypted payroll data, role-based access, and compliance with wage regulations
  • Document Management Secure document storage, version control, and access logging for all company documents
  • Driver Portal Secure driver authentication, data privacy, and mobile application security
12. Policy Compliance and Enforcement

Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all personnel. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to appropriate regulatory authorities.

Enforcement Measures:

  • Security awareness training refresher (first offense)
  • Written warning and mandatory remediation (second offense)
  • Suspension of access privileges (serious violations)
  • Termination of employment (willful or repeated violations)
  • Legal action for criminal violations
13. Exceptions and Variations

Requests for exceptions to this policy must be submitted in writing to the CISO with detailed justification, risk assessment, and proposed compensating controls. All exceptions require approval from the CISO and relevant business unit leader.

Exception Criteria:

  • Business necessity cannot be met through other means
  • Risk is acceptable and properly mitigated
  • Compensating controls are implemented
  • Exception is time-limited with review date
