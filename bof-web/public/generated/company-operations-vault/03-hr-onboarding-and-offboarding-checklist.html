HR Onboarding and Offboarding Checklist
Checklist Overview

This HR Onboarding and Offboarding Checklist provides a comprehensive framework for managing new driver onboarding and employee offboarding processes. Use this checklist to ensure consistent documentation, compliance verification, and smooth transitions for all personnel.

Pre-Employment Requirements
Phase 1: Application and Background Check
  • Employment Application: Complete and submit employment application with required documentation.
  • Background Check Authorization: Obtain signed authorization for MVR and criminal background checks.
  • Drug Test Results: Receive negative DOT drug test results within 30 days of application.
  • Physical Exam: Complete DOT physical examination and receive medical clearance.
  • Employment Eligibility: Verify work authorization and eligibility for employment.
Phase 2: Documentation and Onboarding
Required Documents
  • Driver's License: Valid CDL with appropriate endorsements for assigned vehicle type.
  • Medical Certificate: Current DOT medical certificate on file.
  • Social Security Card: Copy and verify SSN for payroll and I-9 completion.
  • W-4 Form: Complete and submit W-4 and state withholding forms.
  • Direct Deposit Authorization: Set up direct deposit for payroll processing.
  • Emergency Contact: Obtain emergency contact information and preferences.
Equipment Assignment
  • Company Equipment: Assign company vehicle, communication devices, and safety equipment.
  • Uniforms: Provide company uniforms and safety gear as per policy.
  • Vehicle Inspection: Complete pre-assignment vehicle inspection and documentation.
System Access and Training
  • BOF System Access: Create user accounts and assign appropriate permissions.
  • Communication Training: Complete company communication systems training.
  • Safety Training: Complete mandatory safety training and certification.
  • Route Training: Complete route planning and navigation system training.
  • Compliance Training: Complete compliance and regulatory requirements training.
Phase 3: Orientation and Integration
First Day Orientation
  • Company Introduction: Present company mission, values, and culture.
  • Team Introductions: Introduce to immediate team and key contacts.
  • Operations Overview: Review dispatch procedures and communication protocols.
  • Safety Procedures: Review safety protocols and emergency procedures.
  • Equipment Orientation: Review assigned vehicle and equipment operation.
  • Documentation Review: Review required documents and procedures.
First Week Integration
  • Supervised Routes: Complete initial routes with experienced driver supervision.
  • System Proficiency: Demonstrate proficiency with BOF systems and procedures.
  • Documentation Completion: Ensure all required documents are completed and filed.
Phase 4: Compliance Verification
Documentation Audit
  • File Audit: Verify all required documents are complete and properly filed.
  • System Access Review: Confirm appropriate system permissions and access levels.
  • Training Completion: Verify all mandatory training is completed and documented.
90-Day Review
  • Performance Review: Conduct initial 90-day performance evaluation.
  • Compliance Check: Verify ongoing compliance with all policies and procedures.
  • Feedback Session: Provide feedback and address any concerns.
Offboarding Procedures
Resignation Process
  • Written Notice: Receive and process written resignation notice.
  • Exit Interview: Conduct exit interview and document feedback.
  • Final Paycheck: Process final paycheck and settle any advances.
  • Equipment Return: Collect and process all company equipment returns.
System Access Termination
  • Account Deactivation: Deactivate BOF system accounts and access.
  • Document Return: Collect all company documents and property.
  • Final Clearance: Complete clearance process and exit documentation.
Implementation Notes

Version Control: Maintain detailed records of onboarding progress and completion status.

Compliance Tracking: Document all compliance verifications and training completions.

Integration: Connect onboarding process with existing BOF HR and payroll systems.
