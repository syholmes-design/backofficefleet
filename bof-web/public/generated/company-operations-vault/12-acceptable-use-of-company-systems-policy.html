1. Purpose and Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes comprehensive guidelines for appropriate use of BOF's information technology resources, systems, networks, and digital assets. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, drivers, owner-operators, and third parties with access to BOF systems, whether on-premises, remote, or mobile.

Covered Systems: All company-owned or leased computing devices, networks, software applications, cloud services, mobile devices, communication systems, and related infrastructure.

2. General Principles
  • Business Purpose Systems are provided for business purposes and authorized activities only
  • Professional Conduct Use systems professionally and maintain appropriate workplace behavior
  • Security Responsibility Protect systems and data through responsible use practices
  • Compliance Requirement Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies
3. Acceptable Use
  • Business Operations Dispatch, scheduling, routing, fleet management, and transportation logistics
  • Communication Business email, messaging, video conferencing, and team collaboration tools
  • Document Management Access to company documents, forms, business records, and compliance materials
  • Training and Development Access to training materials, professional development resources, and certification programs
  • Productivity Tools Authorized productivity applications, business software, and cloud services
  • Driver Portal Access Self-service access to personal documents, schedules, and communications
4. Prohibited Use
  • Personal Activities Personal business, entertainment, social media browsing, or non-work related activities
  • Illegal Activities Any illegal, unethical, fraudulent, or prohibited activities under applicable laws
  • Unauthorized Software Installation of unauthorized, unlicensed, or unapproved software and applications
  • Data Misuse Unauthorized access, modification, copying, or disclosure of company data
  • System Abuse Excessive resource usage, system interference, or performance degradation
  • Cryptocurrency Mining Cryptocurrency mining, blockchain operations, or distributed computing
  • File Sharing Peer-to-peer file sharing, torrent downloads, or unauthorized data transfers
5. Security Requirements
  • Password Security Complex passwords with multi-factor authentication and no credential sharing
  • Access Control Secure logout procedures and workstation protection when unattended
  • Reporting Requirements Immediate reporting of security incidents, breaches, and policy violations
  • Remote Access Secure remote access with VPN, encryption, and multi-factor authentication
  • Mobile Device Security Device encryption, screen locks, and mobile device management compliance
6. Data Protection and Privacy

Users must protect company and customer data in accordance with BOF's data protection policies and applicable privacy laws.

Data Handling Requirements:

  • Classify data according to BOF's Information Classification Policy
  • Use encryption for sensitive data storage and transmission
  • Follow data retention and disposal procedures
  • Report data breaches immediately to the Security Operations Center
  • Obtain proper authorization before accessing sensitive data
7. Email and Communication Standards

Company communication systems must be used professionally and in compliance with legal requirements.

Communication Guidelines:

  • Professional language and appropriate business communication
  • No harassment, discrimination, or inappropriate content
  • Confidential information only sent to authorized recipients
  • Legal disclaimers included on external communications
  • No unsolicited bulk email or marketing communications
8. Internet and Web Usage

Internet access is provided for business purposes. Personal internet use should be minimal and not interfere with work responsibilities.

Internet Restrictions:

  • No access to illegal, inappropriate, or malicious websites
  • No downloading of unauthorized software or media files
  • No streaming services that consume excessive bandwidth
  • No online gambling or gaming activities
  • Web filtering and monitoring systems are in place
9. Monitoring and Enforcement
  • System Monitoring Authorized monitoring of system usage, network activity, and communications
  • Policy Violations Disciplinary action for violations including warnings, suspension, or termination
  • Investigation Rights Company reserves right to investigate suspected violations and access systems
  • Legal Cooperation Cooperation with law enforcement and regulatory investigations as required
10. Policy Acknowledgment

All users must acknowledge receipt and understanding of this policy as a condition of system access. Violations may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract, and may be reported to appropriate authorities.

User Responsibilities:

  • Read and understand this policy completely
  • Ask questions about unclear provisions
  • Report suspected violations to management or IT security
  • Participate in required security awareness training
  • Maintain awareness of policy updates and changes
