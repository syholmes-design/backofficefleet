Acceptable Use of Company Systems Policy
Policy Overview

This Acceptable Use of Company Systems Policy establishes guidelines for appropriate use of BOF's information technology resources, ensuring security, productivity, and compliance while protecting company assets and data.

General Principles
  • Business Purpose Systems are provided for business purposes and authorized activities only
  • Professional Conduct Use systems professionally and maintain appropriate workplace behavior
  • Security Responsibility Protect systems and data through responsible use practices
  • Compliance Requirement Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies
Acceptable Use
  • Business Operations Dispatch, scheduling, routing, and fleet management activities
  • Communication Business email, messaging, and collaboration tools
  • Document Management Access to company documents, forms, and business records
  • Training and Development Access to training materials and professional development resources
  • Productivity Tools Authorized productivity and business applications
Prohibited Use
  • Personal Activities Personal business, entertainment, or non-work related activities
  • Illegal Activities Any illegal, unethical, or prohibited activities
  • Unauthorized Software Installation of unauthorized or unapproved software
  • Data Misuse Unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure of company data
  • System Abuse Excessive resource usage or system interference
Security Requirements
  • Password Security Strong passwords with regular changes and no sharing
  • Access Control Log out when finished and protect workstations when unattended
  • Reporting Requirements Report security incidents and policy violations immediately
  • Remote Access Secure remote access with VPN and multi-factor authentication
Monitoring and Enforcement
  • System Monitoring Authorized monitoring of system usage and network activity
  • Policy Violations Disciplinary action for policy violations up to termination
  • Investigation Rights Company reserves right to investigate suspected violations
