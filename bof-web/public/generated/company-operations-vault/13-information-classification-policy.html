Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
Information Classification Policy
Policy Overview

This Information Classification Policy establishes a standardized framework for classifying BOF's information assets based on sensitivity, confidentiality requirements, and business impact. It ensures appropriate protection measures are applied to all company information.

Classification Levels
  • Public Information intended for general public distribution, no restrictions
  • Internal Company internal use only, limited to employees and contractors
  • Confidential Sensitive business information, restricted to authorized personnel
  • Restricted Highly sensitive information, need-to-know basis only
Classification Criteria
  • Business Impact Assess potential harm to business operations if compromised
  • Legal Requirements Consider regulatory and contractual obligations
  • Sensitivity Evaluate information sensitivity and privacy implications
  • Distribution Needs Determine who needs access for business functions
Handling Requirements
  • Public Information No special handling required, standard business communication
  • Internal Information Employee access only, no external sharing without authorization
  • Confidential Information Encryption, access controls, and need-to-know distribution
  • Restricted Information Strict access controls, audit trails, and limited distribution
Classification Examples
  • Public Marketing materials, press releases, website content
  • Internal Internal procedures, employee directories, training materials
  • Confidential Financial data, customer lists, strategic plans, contracts
  • Restricted Mergers and acquisitions, legal matters, security incidents
Labeling and Marking
  • Document Labeling All documents must include classification header and footer
  • Electronic Marking Email subjects and file names indicate classification level
  • Physical Marking Confidential documents marked with appropriate labels
Access Control
  • Authorization Access based on job function and classification level
  • Review Process Regular access reviews and immediate revocation when needed
  • Audit Trail Comprehensive logging of access to classified information
