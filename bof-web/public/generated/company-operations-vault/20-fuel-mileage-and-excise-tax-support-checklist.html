Purpose

This checklist provides standardized procedures for documenting fuel purchases, mileage tracking, and excise tax compliance requirements. This checklist ensures proper record-keeping for IFTA reporting, fuel tax calculations, and audit support while maintaining compliance with federal and state regulations.

Scope

This checklist applies to all fuel purchases, mileage tracking, and excise tax reporting activities. It covers:

  • Fuel purchase documentation and receipts
  • Mileage tracking and trip documentation
  • IFTA reporting requirements
  • State fuel tax compliance
  • Audit support documentation

Fuel Purchase Documentation

Fuel Receipt Requirements

  • ☐ Original fuel receipt retained
  • ☐ Date of purchase clearly visible
  • ☐ Fuel station name and location
  • ☐ Number of gallons purchased
  • ☐ Price per gallon
  • ☐ Total amount paid
  • ☐ Payment method (cash, card, account)
  • ☐ Vehicle or equipment identification

Fuel Card Documentation

  • ☐ Fuel card statement retained
  • ☐ Transaction details matched to receipts
  • ☐ Driver identification verified
  • ☐ Vehicle assignment confirmed
  • ☐ Unauthorized transactions flagged
  • ☐ Monthly reconciliation completed

Fuel Quality and Type

  • ☐ Fuel type documented (diesel, gasoline, etc.)
  • ☐ Fuel quality records maintained
  • ☐ Off-road fuel purchases identified
  • ☐ Tax-exempt purchases documented
  • ☐ Bulk fuel purchases properly recorded

Mileage Tracking Documentation

Trip Mileage Records

  • ☐ Origin and destination documented
  • ☐ Total trip miles recorded
  • ☐ Loaded miles documented
  • ☐ Empty miles documented
  • ☐ State-by-state mileage breakdown
  • ☐ Date of travel recorded
  • ☐ Vehicle identification confirmed

Electronic Logging Device (ELD) Records

  • ☐ ELD data downloaded and retained
  • ☐ Mileage accuracy verified
  • ☐ Cross-state mileage confirmed
  • ☐ Driver logs reviewed for completeness
  • ☐ Edit logs documented and justified

Manual Mileage Logs

  • ☐ Manual log sheets retained
  • ☐ Driver signature obtained
  • ☐ Mileage calculations verified
  • ☐ Route documentation attached
  • ☐ Date stamps confirmed

IFTA Reporting Requirements

Quarterly IFTA Reporting

  • ☐ All fuel purchases compiled by quarter
  • ☐ All mileage records compiled by quarter
  • ☐ State-by-state fuel tax calculations completed
  • ☐ IFTA report prepared and filed
  • ☐ Supporting documentation retained
  • ☐ Payment confirmations filed

State Fuel Tax Reporting

  • ☐ Individual state tax returns filed
  • ☐ State-specific requirements met
  • ☐ Quarterly payments made
  • ☐ Annual reconciliations completed
  • ☐ State correspondence retained

Audit Support Documentation

  • ☐ IFTA audit package prepared
  • ☐ State audit documentation organized
  • ☐ Fuel sample tests documented
  • ☐ Mileage verification records maintained
  • ☐ Audit findings addressed

Excise Tax Compliance

Federal Excise Tax Requirements

  • ☐ Form 2290 filed (Heavy Vehicle Use Tax)
  • ☐ Schedule 1 maintained
  • ☐ Vehicle registration documentation
  • ☐ Tax payment confirmations retained
  • ☐ Suspended vehicle documentation

State Excise Tax Requirements

  • ☐ State excise tax filings completed
  • ☐ Weight distance tax documentation
  • ☐ State-specific requirements met
  • ☐ Exemption certificates maintained
  • ☐ Tax payment records retained

Tax Exemption Documentation

  • ☐ Exemption certificates obtained
  • ☐ Off-road fuel purchases documented
  • ☐ Farm use exemptions documented
  • ☐ Government exemptions documented
  • ☐ Exemption justification maintained

Record Retention

Required Retention Periods

  • ☐ Fuel receipts: 7 years
  • ☐ Mileage logs: 7 years
  • ☐ IFTA reports: 7 years
  • ☐ State tax returns: 7 years
  • ☐ Excise tax filings: 7 years
  • ☐ Audit documentation: 7 years

Record Organization

  • ☐ Records organized by year
  • ☐ Records organized by quarter
  • ☐ Records organized by state
  • ☐ Records organized by vehicle
  • ☐ Electronic backup maintained
  • ☐ Physical copies secured

Quality Control

Monthly Reviews

  • ☐ Fuel receipt completeness verified
  • ☐ Mileage log accuracy confirmed
  • ☐ Fuel card reconciliation completed
  • ☐ ELD data download verified
  • ☐ Exception documentation reviewed

Quarterly Reviews

  • ☐ IFTA reporting accuracy verified
  • ☐ State tax compliance confirmed
  • ☐ Excise tax filings reviewed
  • ☐ Audit documentation updated

Annual Reviews

  • ☐ Complete compliance assessment
  • ☐ Process efficiency evaluation
  • ☐ Technology utilization review
  • ☐ Training needs assessment
  • ☐ Policy updates implemented

Fuel Tax & Excise Audit Support Requirements

  • ☐ Mileage by jurisdiction documented
  • ☐ Fuel purchases by jurisdiction tracked
  • ☐ Tax-paid fuel support available
  • ☐ All fuel receipts retained
  • ☐ Route records complete
  • ☐ Asset/unit assignments documented
  • ☐ Quarterly review completed
  • ☐ Audit response notes maintained
  • ☐ BOF estimate vs assessment comparison
  • ☐ Variance documentation available
