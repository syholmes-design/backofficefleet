Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc.
Employee Handbook
1. Welcome and Company Overview

Welcome to Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. This handbook serves as your guide to our company policies, procedures, and expectations. As a member of our team, you are essential to our success in providing safe, reliable, and professional transportation services.

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. is a professional carrier specializing in freight transportation and logistics services. We operate with a commitment to safety, integrity, and customer service excellence. Our fleet serves customers across regional and long-haul routes, maintaining the highest standards of operational excellence.

This handbook is designed to help you understand your role, responsibilities, and the resources available to you. Please read it carefully and keep it accessible for reference throughout your employment.

2. Mission, Operating Principles, and Professional Standards

Mission: To provide exceptional transportation services through safe operations, professional conduct, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and reliability.

Operating Principles:

  • Safety First: We prioritize safety in all operations, decisions, and actions.
  • Integrity: We conduct business honestly and ethically in all interactions.
  • Professionalism: We maintain professional appearance, conduct, and communication.
  • Customer Focus: We exceed customer expectations through reliable service.
  • Teamwork: We collaborate and support each other to achieve common goals.
  • Continuous Improvement: We seek opportunities to enhance skills and processes.

Professional Standards: All employees are expected to represent Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. with professionalism at all times. This includes appropriate attire, courteous communication, punctuality, and adherence to company policies and procedures.

3. Equal Employment Opportunity and Non-Discrimination

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. is an equal opportunity employer. We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where all employees are treated with respect and dignity. All employment decisions, including recruitment, hiring, promotion, training, and compensation, are based on qualifications, merit, and business needs.

We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including hiring, placement, promotion, termination, layoff, recall, transfer, leaves of absence, compensation, and training.

4. Anti-Harassment and Workplace Respect

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. is committed to maintaining a work environment free from harassment, discrimination, and intimidation. We expect all employees to treat each other with respect and professionalism.

Harassment based on any protected characteristic is strictly prohibited. This includes unwelcome conduct, comments, jokes, or physical contact that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

Employees who experience or witness harassment should immediately report it to management or HR. All complaints will be investigated promptly and confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment in good faith is prohibited.

5. Employment Classification

Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. employs various classifications of workers to meet our operational needs. Each classification has specific rights, responsibilities, and benefits.

  • Company Drivers: Full-time or part-time employees who operate company-owned vehicles. Eligible for company benefits including health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off.
  • Owner-Operators / Independent Contractors: Self-employed individuals who own and operate their own equipment under contract with Delta Advanced Trucking, Inc. Responsible for their own taxes, insurance, and benefits.
  • Office Staff: Administrative, dispatch, safety, and management personnel who support operations. Eligible for full company benefits and subject to standard employment policies.
  • Temporary or Contract Personnel: Workers hired through agencies or on a temporary basis to meet specific needs. Not eligible for company benefits unless otherwise specified.

Employment classification will be clearly defined in your employment agreement or contract. Misclassification of employment status is prohibited.

6. Driver Qualification and Compliance Requirements

All drivers must meet and maintain qualification requirements as mandated by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) and company standards.

  • Commercial Driver's License (CDL): Valid CDL appropriate for vehicle type and endorsements required.
  • Medical Card: Current DOT medical certificate maintained on file and carried while operating commercial vehicles.
  • Motor Vehicle Record (MVR): Clean driving record meeting company and FMCSA standards.
  • FMCSA/DOT Compliance: Compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local regulations.
  • Drug and Alcohol Testing: Participation in required drug and alcohol testing programs where applicable.
  • Document Renewal Responsibility: Drivers are responsible for timely renewal of all required documents and licenses.

Failure to maintain required qualifications may result in suspension of driving privileges or termination of employment.

7. Attendance, Availability, and Dispatch Communication

Regular attendance and availability are essential for meeting customer commitments and maintaining operational efficiency.

  • Punctuality: Report to work on time as scheduled. Notify dispatch of any delays immediately.
  • Availability: Maintain availability according to your work schedule and on-call requirements.
  • Communication: Maintain regular communication with dispatch regarding location, status, and any issues.
  • Call-in Procedures: Follow established procedures for reporting absences or requesting time off.
  • Emergency Contact: Keep emergency contact information current and notify dispatch of personal emergencies.
8. Safety, Accident Reporting, and Incident Escalation

Safety is our highest priority. All employees must follow safety procedures and report incidents immediately.

  • Safety Procedures: Follow all company safety policies and procedures without exception.
  • Accident Reporting: Report all accidents, incidents, and near-misses immediately to dispatch and safety management.
  • Incident Documentation: Complete required incident reports and provide accurate information.
  • Emergency Response: Follow established procedures for emergency situations and medical emergencies.
  • Safety Training: Participate in required safety training and maintain current certifications.
9. Vehicle, Equipment, Cargo, and Seal Responsibilities

Drivers are responsible for the proper care, operation, and security of company vehicles, equipment, and cargo.

  • Vehicle Care: Maintain cleanliness and perform basic vehicle checks and maintenance.
  • Equipment Operation: Use all equipment properly and report any malfunctions immediately.
  • Cargo Security: Ensure cargo is properly loaded, secured, and protected from damage or theft.
  • Seal Integrity: Verify and maintain seal integrity throughout transit. Report any seal issues immediately.
  • Damage Reporting: Report any vehicle, equipment, or cargo damage promptly and accurately.
10. Pre-Trip, In-Transit, and Post-Trip Documentation

Accurate and timely documentation is essential for compliance, customer service, and operational efficiency.

  • Pre-Trip Inspection: Complete thorough pre-trip vehicle inspections and documentation.
  • In-Transit Updates: Provide regular status updates and maintain electronic logs as required.
  • Post-Trip Reports: Complete post-trip inspections and submit required documentation.
  • Bill of Lading: Ensure accurate completion and proper handling of all shipping documents.
  • Proof of Delivery: Obtain and document proper proof of delivery for all shipments.
11. Electronic Systems, BOF Platform Use, and Data Accuracy

BOF serves as our primary operating platform for dispatch, documentation, and operational management.

  • System Access: Use BOF platform according to assigned permissions and company policies.
  • Data Accuracy: Ensure all data entered into BOF is accurate, complete, and timely.
  • Electronic Logs: Maintain electronic logging devices (ELDs) and logs as required by regulations.
  • System Security: Protect login credentials and report any security concerns immediately.
  • Training: Complete required training on BOF platform and related electronic systems.
12. Payroll, Settlements, Deductions, and Reimbursements

Compensation and settlements are processed according to established schedules and company policies.

  • Pay Schedule: Payroll is processed according to the established company pay schedule.
  • Settlement Processing: Settlements are processed after completion of required documentation.
  • Deductions: Authorized deductions will be made as required by law or company policy.
  • Reimbursements: Submit expense reimbursements with proper documentation and approval.
  • Pay Statements: Review pay statements regularly and report any discrepancies promptly.
13. Expense, Lumper, Fuel, Toll, and Accessorial Documentation

Proper documentation of expenses and accessorial charges is essential for accurate reimbursement and billing.

  • Expense Reports: Submit expense reports with original receipts and proper documentation.
  • Lumper Fees: Obtain proper documentation and authorization for lumper fees.
  • Fuel Purchases: Use company fuel cards and maintain proper fuel purchase documentation.
  • Toll Reimbursement: Submit toll receipts with proper documentation for reimbursement.
  • Accessorial Charges: Document all accessorial services with proper authorization and receipts.
14. Claims, Cargo Damage, and Insurance Cooperation

Prompt reporting and cooperation are essential for effective claims management and insurance coverage.

  • Incident Reporting: Report all incidents, accidents, and cargo damage immediately.
  • Evidence Preservation: Preserve all evidence including photos, documents, and witness information.
  • Claims Cooperation: Cooperate fully with claims investigations and insurance adjusters.
  • Documentation: Complete all required claims documentation accurately and promptly.
  • Preventive Measures: Follow procedures to prevent future incidents and cargo damage.
15. Confidentiality and Company Records

Company information and records are confidential and must be protected according to company policies.

  • Confidential Information: Maintain confidentiality of all company and customer information.
  • Record Access: Access company records only as required for job duties and with proper authorization.
  • Document Security: Protect all company documents from unauthorized access or disclosure.
  • Return of Records: Return all company records and documents upon separation from employment.
  • Social Media: Do not share confidential company information on social media or public platforms.
16. Mobile Device, Camera, Dashcam, and Telematics Policy

Electronic devices and recording equipment are provided for safety, compliance, and operational purposes.

  • Mobile Device Use: Use mobile devices only when safely parked and in compliance with regulations.
  • Camera Equipment: Use company-provided cameras for documentation and safety purposes.
  • Dashcam Operation: Ensure dashcam equipment is operational and not tampered with or disabled.
  • Telematics Data: Telematics data is used for safety, compliance, and operational management.
  • Personal Devices: Personal electronic devices must not interfere with job duties or safety.
17. Anti-Retaliation and Reporting Concerns

Employees are encouraged to report concerns without fear of retaliation or adverse consequences.

  • Reporting Process: Report concerns through appropriate channels including management, HR, or compliance.
  • Anti-Retaliation: Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is prohibited.
  • Investigation: All reported concerns will be investigated promptly and confidentially.
  • Protection: Employees who report concerns are protected from retaliation and adverse employment actions.
  • Good Faith Reporting: Reports must be made in good faith with reasonable basis for the concern.
18. Discipline, Corrective Action, and Coaching

Progressive discipline and coaching are used to address performance and conduct issues.

  • Progressive Discipline: Discipline typically follows a progressive approach from verbal warning to termination.
  • Corrective Action: Focus on correcting behavior and improving performance through coaching and training.
  • Documentation: All disciplinary actions will be documented in the employee's personnel file.
  • Immediate Termination: Certain serious violations may result in immediate termination without prior warnings.
  • Opportunity to Improve: Employees will be given reasonable opportunity to correct performance issues.
19. Separation, Final Pay, and Return of Company Property

Proper procedures must be followed when employment ends to ensure smooth transition and final settlement.

  • Notice Period: Provide appropriate notice according to employment agreement or company policy.
  • Exit Process: Participate in exit interview and complete required separation documentation.
  • Property Return: Return all company property including keys, equipment, documents, and uniforms.
  • Final Pay: Final pay will be processed according to applicable laws and company policy.
  • Benefits Continuation: Information about benefits continuation options will be provided upon request.
