Payroll, Compensation, and Deductions Policy
Policy Overview

This Payroll, Compensation, and Deductions Policy establishes the framework for compensation structures, payroll processing, and deduction administration across BOF. It ensures compliance with federal and state regulations while providing clear guidelines for all payroll-related transactions.

Compensation Structure

BOF offers competitive compensation packages based on experience, performance, and market conditions. All compensation decisions follow established pay scales and are documented in driver agreements.

Pay Schedule

Drivers are paid bi-weekly on Fridays via direct deposit. Pay periods run from Monday through Sunday, with payroll processing completed by Wednesday each week.

Overtime Policy

Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate for hours worked over 40 in a workweek. All overtime must be pre-approved by dispatch and documented in payroll records.

Deduction Administration

All payroll deductions require proper authorization and documentation. Deductions are processed per federal and state guidelines, with detailed records maintained for each pay period.

Deduction Limits and Review

Deductions exceeding 30% of gross pay require additional review and approval. Large deduction ratios may indicate calculation errors, unauthorized deductions, or special circumstances that need verification before settlement release.

Review Required: Any deduction total greater than 30% of gross pay must be reviewed by payroll management to ensure accuracy and proper authorization.

Family Support Withholding

Family support and child support withholding is processed per court orders and state agency requirements. Amounts are calculated based on court-ordered percentages and may vary by state. All family support withholdings are documented in Phase 5 Payroll Garnishment Withholding Summaries.

Important: Family support withholdings take priority over other deductions and are protected by federal law. Changes to withholding amounts require updated court orders.
Benefits Administration

BOF offers comprehensive benefits including health, dental, vision, life insurance, and flexible spending accounts. Benefits administration follows ERISA guidelines and includes annual enrollment periods.

Payroll Processing

Payroll is processed through secure systems with proper segregation of duties. All payroll records are maintained for 7 years and available for employee review upon request.

Compliance and Reporting

This policy complies with all applicable federal and state labor laws. Regular audits ensure continued compliance and accuracy of payroll processing.
