Lease Terms

Term: This Agreement shall commence on October 15, 2025, and continue for an initial term of 12 months unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

Rent: Company shall pay Owner-Operator a weekly lease payment of $775.00 for use of the Equipment.

Payment Schedule: Weekly payments shall be made via direct deposit every Friday for the preceding week.

Owner-Operator Responsibilities

Owner-Operator shall:

  • Maintain Equipment in good working condition and compliance with all DOT regulations
  • Provide all routine maintenance, repairs, and inspections
  • Maintain required insurance coverage on Equipment
  • Keep Equipment clean and presentable
  • Provide fuel for Equipment operation
  • Comply with all FMCSA regulations
  • Maintain proper licensing and certifications
Company Responsibilities

Company shall:

  • Provide dispatch services and load assignments
  • Handle billing and collections for services rendered
  • Provide fuel cards and advance programs
  • Maintain cargo insurance coverage
  • Provide regulatory compliance support
  • Pay weekly lease payments as agreed
Insurance Requirements

Owner-Operator shall maintain at all times:

  • Commercial auto liability insurance: $1,000,000 CSL
  • Physical damage coverage on Equipment: Actual cash value
  • General liability insurance: $1,000,000 CSL
  • Workers' compensation or occupational accident coverage
  • Company named as loss payee and additional insured
Maintenance and Repairs

Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all routine maintenance and repairs, including:

  • Oil changes and preventive maintenance
  • Tire replacement and repairs
  • Brake service and repairs
  • Engine and transmission repairs
  • DOT inspections and compliance
  • All other maintenance and repair costs
Fuel and Expenses

Fuel: Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all fuel costs. Company shall provide fuel cards with established credit limits and discounts.

Tolls and Fees: Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all tolls, permits, and fees unless otherwise agreed.

Advances: Company may provide fuel advances and expense advances subject to established policies and repayment terms.

Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice. Company may terminate immediately for cause, including:

  • Breach of safety regulations
  • Failure to maintain required insurance
  • Equipment damage due to negligence
  • Violation of drug and alcohol policies
  • Non-compliance with DOT regulations
Indemnification

Owner-Operator shall indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from Owner-Operator's operation of the Equipment, except to the extent caused by Company's negligence.
