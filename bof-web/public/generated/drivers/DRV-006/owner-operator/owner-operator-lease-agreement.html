5. Term and Termination
5.1 Term

This Agreement shall continue for the initial term and any renewal terms unless terminated earlier as provided herein.

5.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days written notice to the other party.

5.3 Termination for Cause

Company may terminate immediately for cause, including but not limited to:

  • Breach of safety regulations or DOT compliance
  • Failure to maintain required insurance coverage
  • Equipment damage due to negligence or misuse
  • Violation of drug and alcohol policies
  • Material breach of any provision of this Agreement
  • Revocation or suspension of Owner-Operator's commercial driver's license
6. Compensation and Payment Terms
6.1 Weekly Lease Payment

Company shall pay Owner-Operator a weekly lease payment of $750.00 for use of the Equipment.

6.2 Payment Schedule

Weekly payments shall be made via Weekly Direct Deposit every Friday for the preceding week's services.

6.3 Itemized Settlement Documentation

Company shall provide Owner-Operator with detailed settlement statements showing all revenue, deductions, and lease payments for each pay period.

7. Owner-Operator Responsibilities

Owner-Operator shall be responsible for:

  • Maintaining Equipment in good working condition and compliance with all DOT regulations
  • Providing all routine maintenance, repairs, and inspections
  • Maintaining required insurance coverage on Equipment as specified in Section 8
  • Keeping Equipment clean and presentable at all times
  • Providing fuel for Equipment operation and maintaining fuel receipts
  • Complying with all FMCSA regulations and hours of service requirements
  • Maintaining proper licensing, certifications, and medical qualifications
  • Completing all required paperwork and documentation in a timely manner
  • Notifying Company immediately of any Equipment issues or accidents
8. Company Responsibilities

Company shall be responsible for:

  • Providing dispatch services and load assignments
  • Handling billing and collections for services rendered
  • Providing fuel cards with established credit limits and discounts
  • Maintaining cargo insurance coverage for transported goods
  • Providing regulatory compliance support and documentation
  • Paying weekly lease payments as agreed in Section 6
  • Providing settlement statements and support documentation
  • Maintaining necessary operating authority and permits
9. Insurance Requirements

Owner-Operator shall maintain at all times the following minimum insurance coverage:

  • Commercial Auto Liability: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit
  • Physical Damage Coverage: Actual cash value of Equipment with $1,000 deductible
  • General Liability: $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit
  • Workers' Compensation: As required by law or Occupational Accident Coverage
  • Cargo Insurance: $100,000 per occurrence, $500,000 in aggregate
  • Company named as loss payee and additional insured on all policies
  • Certificates of insurance provided to Company upon request
10. Maintenance and Repairs
10.1 Owner-Operator Responsibilities

Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all routine maintenance and repairs, including:

  • Oil changes and preventive maintenance at recommended intervals
  • Tire replacement, rotation, and repairs
  • Brake service and repairs
  • Engine and transmission repairs and overhauls
  • DOT annual inspections and compliance maintenance
  • All other maintenance and repair costs necessary to keep Equipment in good working order
10.2 Maintenance Records

Owner-Operator shall maintain complete maintenance records and make them available to Company upon request.

11. Fuel, Toll, and Operating Expenses
11.1 Fuel

Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all fuel costs. Company shall provide fuel cards with established credit limits and negotiated discounts.

11.2 Tolls and Fees

Owner-Operator shall be responsible for all tolls, permits, and fees unless otherwise agreed in writing.

11.3 Advances

Company may provide fuel advances and expense advances subject to established policies and repayment terms as specified in the Settlement/Payment Authorization.

12. Compliance and Safety

Owner-Operator shall comply with all applicable federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and industry standards, including but not limited to:

  • All FMCSA regulations and hours of service requirements
  • DOT drug and alcohol testing requirements
  • Vehicle inspection and maintenance standards
  • Hazardous materials regulations (if applicable)
  • Company safety policies and procedures
  • Electronic logging device (ELD) requirements and compliance
13. Indemnification

Owner-Operator shall indemnify and hold Company harmless from any claims, damages, losses, or liabilities arising from Owner-Operator's operation of the Equipment, except to the extent caused by Company's negligence or willful misconduct.

14. Return of Company Property

Upon termination of this Agreement, Owner-Operator shall immediately return all Company property, including but not limited to fuel cards, dispatch equipment, and any other items provided by Company.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

This Agreement may only be modified by written agreement signed by both parties.
