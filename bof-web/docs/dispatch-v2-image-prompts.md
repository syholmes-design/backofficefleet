# Dispatch v2 Image Prompts

Create 9 realistic, crop-ready BOF dispatch pre-trip photo assets.

## Global Style

Realistic long-haul trucking operations photography, premium fleet documentation style, clean commercial truck, no visible real company logos, no readable DOT numbers, no readable license plates, no copyrighted branding, natural daylight, high-resolution, realistic textures, professional safety/compliance documentation feel. Images should be easy to crop for a web app card. Leave some clean negative space for UI overlays.

Output folder:

`public/generated/dispatch-v2/pretrip/`

## 1. driver-with-cdl.jpg

A professional truck driver standing beside an open semi-truck cab door, holding a CDL-style credential card toward the camera. The card should look generic and unreadable, not an actual government ID. Driver in clean safety vest, truck cab visible, compliance documentation style, crop-friendly.

## 2. driver-selfie-dashcam.jpg

Interior cab view from a dash camera perspective showing a professional driver seated in the driver seat, face clearly visible, hands off wheel, safe parked position, dashboard softly visible, no readable brand logos, realistic lighting.

## 3. truck-front.jpg

Full front view of a modern semi-truck parked in a freight yard, clean grille, headlights visible, license plate blurred or unreadable, symmetrical composition, enough space around the truck for cropping.

## 4. truck-driver-side.jpg

Full driver-side profile of a modern sleeper cab tractor with attached trailer in a logistics yard, clean inspection-photo style, wheels and side mirrors visible, no branding, crop-friendly horizontal composition.

## 5. truck-passenger-side.jpg

Full passenger-side profile of a modern sleeper cab tractor with trailer, parked on clean pavement, inspection documentation style, no logos, no readable numbers, crop-friendly.

## 6. fifth-wheel-coupling.jpg

Close-up of fifth wheel coupling and kingpin lock area between tractor and trailer, clean mechanical detail, realistic grease/metal texture, safety inspection perspective, no text overlays.

## 7. trailer-rear-seal.jpg

Rear view of closed trailer doors with a visible generic numbered seal area, seal should be visually obvious but not contain readable real numbers, clean freight yard background, compliance proof photo style.

## 8. dashboard-eld-screen.jpg

Truck dashboard and ELD/tablet screen visible from driver seat perspective, generic ELD interface with unreadable details, odometer area visible but not readable, parked and safe, modern cab.

## 9. fuel-receipt.jpg
